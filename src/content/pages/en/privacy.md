Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve, measure, or improve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other websites. Visitors can manage personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and read [Google's explanation of how AdSense uses cookies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7549925?hl=en).

This site discloses advertising cookies and related data processing as required for Google publishers. Cookie, personalized-advertising, and consent requirements vary by region; Google may also provide controls through AdSense Privacy & messaging.

## Email contact

If you contact me by email, I receive your email address, message, and any other information you choose to include. I use it only to read, reply to, and handle your request, and I do not sell it to third parties. Messages are deleted when they are no longer needed for the request; system backups may retain them for longer.

You may ask me to delete contact records that I still control. I may retain the minimum information needed for legal, dispute-resolution, or security purposes.

## External links

Posts and pages may link to GitHub, Google, social networks, vendor documentation, or other third-party websites. Once you open an external link, that site's privacy policy and cookie settings govern its data handling. This site cannot control those practices.

If the analytics tools, advertising services, or contact channels used by this site change, I will update this page and its date. Continuing to use the site means you have had the opportunity to read this notice.
