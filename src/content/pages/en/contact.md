## Get in touch

You can email [haogre@gmail.com](mailto:haogre@gmail.com).

You are welcome to write about a post, report a broken page or link, share technical material, or suggest a collaboration related to the subjects covered here. For technical questions, including the operating system, software version, and any error details you can share publicly will make it easier to help.

There is no contact form on this site, so the site does not collect form fields, uploaded files, or user accounts. Email is handled only as described in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
