## Who I am

I am HAOGRE. This is my personal blog: a place where I turn technical practice, reading, family life, and persistent curiosity into notes that can be revisited later.

## What I write about

- **Technical practice:** systems, networking, home servers, programming tools, and problems I have investigated myself.
- **AI and business:** AI products, engineering delivery, business models, and the ways people and technology work together.
- **Life and creative work:** family, education, games, reading, films, and stories that are still taking shape.

Most posts come from real use, hands-on experiments, or extended reflection. I try to make the process, evidence, and limitations clear. When an article relies on outside material, I keep source links and correct known errors when I find them.

## About the opinions here

These are personal notes and do not represent an employer, product, or organization. Technical commands and configurations should be tested in your own environment. Health, financial, and legal posts are not a substitute for professional advice.

If you spot an error, want to discuss a post, or have a suitable collaboration, please use the [contact page](/en/contact/). Privacy details are available in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
