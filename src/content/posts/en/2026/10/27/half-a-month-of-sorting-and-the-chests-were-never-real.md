Steve’s War God’s Blade was gone.

At first it had stopped working. Later, after searching everywhere, he could not even find the blade.

He went back to punching trees.

This Minecraft adventure seemed to have taken him into another world, with another set of rules. Before he could work them out, a panel appeared in front of him.

It was called the Three Kingdoms Summoner.

The description said it could summon figures from the Three Kingdoms to help him.

Steve looked down.

Every slot was empty.

He closed the panel and sat in his little house, sighing.

## They could come, but they also had to leave

Eventually, the summoner became useful.

Zhang Fei came and had a drink. Just as Steve began to feel he had someone to help him, the man vanished.

Steve opened the panel again. This time he read all the rules.

Each summon lasted twenty-four hours. Then the summoned figure returned, and another summon required a month’s wait.

Steve looked at the field outside, then at the cooldown.

He could grow quite a lot in a month.

He received some wheat and kept farming. He even ate a melon grown from seeds someone had dropped.

When the wait finally ended, the village held a gathering.

Nobody asked Steve to fight monsters.

They asked him to perform a talent.

Steve stood up. His mind was emptier than the summoner’s original panel.

After a while, he remembered something.

The panel was no longer empty.

He summoned Diaochan, who rescued him from the performance.

The next day, several hundred bandits arrived at the village gate. Steve looked at the summoner he had just used and sighed.

Before he had finished sighing, the village iron golem had settled the matter, one punch at a time.

When twenty-four hours were up, Diaochan returned as well.

Steve closed the panel and picked up his hoe.

“Back to farming.”

## Finishing the fight did not finish the job

Another month passed. This time several thousand bandits arrived, all wearing diamond armor.

Steve could not immediately decide whom to call. He said Huang Zhong’s name almost at random.

Less than half a minute later, the village entrance was quiet.

Several thousand sets of diamond equipment lay on the ground.

Steve was delighted.

He collected a load and took it home. Then another. He filled one chest and found the next. Armor and tools needed separate places, and everything needed counting afterward.

The next day, he was still sorting.

On the fifth day, he was still sorting.

After half a month, Steve finally understood: Huang Zhong had only handled the first half-minute.

The rest of the work was in the chests.

Later, Steve found a diamond sword with a custom “Wind Force Fifteen” effect. It looked impressive. When tens of thousands of iron-armored bandits actually arrived, he still could not beat them.

This time he called Guan Yu, who finished in less than an hour.

Steve looked at the iron armor on the ground. The chests he had just organized already seemed full again.

## No fight left, but plenty of chores

With nothing else to do, he opened the panel. His finger slipped, and he summoned Lü Bu.

The fighting was already over.

The twenty-four-hour timer had already started.

Lü Bu did not sit idle. He helped put things away and tackled one chore after another. Jobs Steve had planned to do gradually kept disappearing from his list.

By the time Lü Bu returned, Steve could finally rest.

The village held another gathering.

Steve checked the summoner first. The cooldown was still running. If anyone asked him to perform this time, he could not call Diaochan.

When he arrived, he discovered that everyone was simply eating melon.

Nobody called him up to perform.

Afterward, he let out a long breath.

“They finally gave me a break.”

Back home, Steve comfortably counted his possessions. The equipment had its proper place. Everything he had sorted was in its chest. Next, he could plant melons.

This time, he even knew what he would do tomorrow.

## One last item he could not record

A few days later, Steve woke to find that he was still in the world of _Annoying Villagers_.

He had never truly entered another world. Someone had placed him inside a trial.

The summons, the farming, the loot—even the chests he had so carefully organized—had all belonged to it.

Steve watched the things in front of him begin to fade. For a while, he said nothing.

Then he went to argue with whoever had arranged the trial.

“You let me spend half a month sorting?”

The organizer was at a loss too. It had been meant to train him.

Steve only wanted to grow melons now.

After some argument, he actually got a chance to do that.

He picked up his hoe and was about to start planting when someone called out again.

“Still lost in the game? Get up! We have to keep fighting HIM!”

Steve looked down at the hoe.

He had not swung it even once this time.

He leaned it aside and took one last look at the soil.

The counting was over.

The melons would have to wait.
