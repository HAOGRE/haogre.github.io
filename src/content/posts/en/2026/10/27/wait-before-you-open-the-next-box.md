Steve had barely found his footing on the grass when a chest fell from the sky.

Alex had no time to speak. Pangpang had no time to see what it was. All three were sent straight back to spawn.

This new Minecraft world had custom rules. Other players started by punching a tree. They started by getting hit by a chest.

## The first chest was worth coming back for

After respawning, the three kept their distance.

The chest did not move.

Steve approached from the side and opened the lid.

Inside was a stack of diamonds.

Pangpang came closer, looked at the diamonds, then at the spot where they had been standing.

“Was it trying to help us or hit us?”

Steve could not tell either.

## The second chest was best opened from a distance

Something else began to fall.

This time they moved aside and waited for the chest to land before opening it.

There were no diamonds inside.

There was a foul-smelling lump of dung.

Pangpang was crafting tools nearby and had missed the reveal. By the time he picked up the lump and mischievously tossed it, Alex was right in front of him.

A moment later, Alex was chasing Steve halfway down the street.

Steve looked back as he ran.

“Wasn’t Pangpang the one who threw it?”

The footsteps behind him did not stop.

For the moment, he could not stop either.

## The third time, the opener got something

When the third delivery landed, Steve and Alex both looked at Pangpang.

Pangpang went over and opened it.

Inside was one enchanted golden apple.

His movements were perfectly clear this time: take it out, put it in his own inventory, close the lid.

Steve and Alex walked over to find an empty box.

“Only one?” Steve asked.

Pangpang nodded.

“What about us?”

Pangpang looked at the empty box again.

He was already heading toward the swamp.

## The fourth time, the first to grab it went back first

Something else fell from the sky.

Pangpang was still in the swamp and had not made it back.

Steve and Alex rushed over. After all those chests, empty boxes, and chases, surely it was their turn.

It was a potion.

Alex reached it first and hurried to drink it.

Steve was about to look for the effect when there was nobody left in front of him.

The potion had triggered a special effect in this world, sending Alex straight back to spawn.

When Pangpang arrived, Steve was still standing there.

“Where is she?”

Steve pointed toward spawn.

Pangpang checked his inventory. The enchanted golden apple was still there.

Steve looked at him.

“You didn’t grab this one.”

Pangpang nodded.

“You ran faster.”
