After several mishaps, Steve decided they should mine for a while.

Alex agreed.

Pangpang did not object either.

Compared with more fighting, at least the stone underground would not actively chase them.

## Plenty of material, very little durability

Their luck was surprisingly good.

The three found a large diamond deposit and soon each made a set of diamond armor.

Steve put his on. Alex did too. Pangpang looked down at his new equipment without hurrying.

Even the equipment rules were altered in this custom Minecraft world.

The new armor had just one point of durability.

Pangpang checked again.

Still one.

The diamond color was bright. The durability bar was barely there.

“Brand new?” he asked.

“Brand new,” Steve said.

Neither said anything else.

## The same little room

A little farther along, Steve realized someone was missing.

Alex looked behind them.

Pangpang had disappeared.

The two turned back to search.

Pangpang had already followed the concealed passage to his little base. It was the same room he had expanded into the stone layer after hiding in a three-block-deep hole with the top block replaced.

The bed was still there, along with the chest, campfire, and torches.

Pangpang stepped sideways around the foot of the bed and reached the chest.

He placed the diamond armor in the original chest, closed the lid, and changed into leather armor.

The color was ordinary.

The durability was full.

## Found, minus a layer of blue

Steve and Alex finally found him beneath a tree.

Pangpang stood there in leather armor, looking calm.

They had been searching for their missing teammate.

Now that they had found him, something still seemed to be missing.

Steve looked down at his own diamond armor, then at Pangpang’s.

“You changed?”

Pangpang nodded.

“This set still has durability.”

Steve checked his own equipment again.

So did his.

One point.

Pangpang did not mention the underground chest. The three continued on their way. Nobody was in a hurry to find a monster and test anything.

At least Pangpang would not spend this trip worrying about that single point.
