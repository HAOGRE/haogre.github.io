The three had only just entered a new world when they defeated a zombie together.

It dropped one iron ingot.

Pangpang picked it up. Steve took a look.

“Tools first.”

“A sword?” Alex asked.

Steve placed the ingot on the crafting table.

“A shovel.”

## Dig it up, then hand it out

The iron shovel went to Pangpang.

Drops and metalworking followed unusual rules in this modded Minecraft world. Pangpang casually dug into the ground and uncovered gold and iron ingots.

The three spent a while working with the metal.

Alex received a golden sword. Pangpang received a shield as well.

With a shovel in one hand and a shield in the other, Pangpang looked down.

Ever since that first iron ingot had landed in his hands, things seemed to be improving.

## It looked like diamond, but it had sugar on it

Before long, they found another chest.

Inside were three pieces of sugar.

Steve and Alex looked at the sugar, then at Pangpang’s iron shovel and shield.

Both pieces of equipment went into the furnace next.

Pangpang stood beside it empty-handed, watching his new equipment melt and finally become a single iron blade.

A layer of sugar coated the blade, making it shine.

From a distance, it almost looked like diamond.

Pangpang leaned closer.

“A diamond blade?”

Steve shook his head.

“Iron.”

Alex added,

“Sugar on the outside.”

Pangpang sniffed it.

Definitely sweet.

## Only afterward did they remember to assign it

Halfway along the path, the three stopped again.

Alex already had a golden sword. Should this iron blade go to Steve or Pangpang?

The blade rested between them for a while.

Steve looked at Pangpang.

Pangpang looked at the blade.

Eventually, they agreed: Pangpang would take it.

He accepted it and tested its weight. The blade was new, the sugar coating intact, and it shone much more brightly in his hand.

Out of habit, he reached with his other hand to pick up his shield.

That hand stopped in midair.

The shield was already in the blade.

He looked down at his waist.

So was the shovel.

“Shall we go?” Steve asked.

Pangpang lowered his empty hand.

“Yes. But next time we need to dig, I’ll have to borrow a shovel.”
