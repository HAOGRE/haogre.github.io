Pangpang’s secret base began as a hole for hiding from Steve.

It started with a box of golden apples.

## A hiding place became somewhere to stay

In another Minecraft world with custom rules, the three had just respawned when they spotted what looked like equipment at a distant camp.

By the time they arrived, the equipment had burned away.

Only a small shulker box in a corner remained. Pangpang mined it up first and put the golden apples inside into his own inventory.

Steve chased him for thousands of blocks.

Tired of running, Pangpang dug down three blocks, jumped in, and replaced the top block above his head.

Steve could not find him from the surface.

Then several fireworks streaked across the sky. A player in gold armor landed and sent Steve back to spawn with one mace strike.

Pangpang went unnoticed underground.

After the three met again, a secure storage slot Pangpang had discovered caught Steve’s attention. It was a feature of this world: golden apples stored there would remain with him after respawning.

Steve looked at the apples and still wanted a share.

Pangpang ran again.

He used the same hiding trick. Steve remained above ground, and the player in gold armor came back once more.

When everything went quiet above him, Pangpang did not leave immediately.

He dug farther down, reached stone, and widened the space. A bed, a chest, a campfire, and torches all fitted if he packed them closely. Finally, he left a concealed passage.

A temporary hiding hole had become a tiny base known only to him.

## On the first visit, he missed it

After another reunion, Pangpang dug a large pit, covered it with grass, and had Steve stand nearby.

The player in gold armor came after him, just as expected.

But he defeated Steve, turned around, and left.

The trap was still there.

Steve was not.

Pangpang looked at the pit, then in the direction the other player had gone.

There had been rather more preparation this time. The result was the same.

## On the second visit, he wanted something cheap

The player in gold armor flew away, then suddenly turned back.

He had forgotten to collect Steve’s wooden pickaxe.

That wooden pickaxe.

On returning, he fell straight into the pit beneath the grass. His health bar emptied at once.

Pangpang hurried over.

The pit now held a set of gold armor, a diamond sword, a shield, and some food.

Steve standing there had not made the player stay.

A wooden pickaxe had now made him leave an entire set of equipment behind.

Pangpang gathered it all, slipped into the concealed passage, and stored everything in the chest in his original little base.

## Steve was still looking for answers

Pangpang returned empty-handed.

Steve had no idea who had taken the equipment. When he learned that the player in gold armor had respawned too, he went over to taunt him.

The other player did not tolerate it for long.

One sword strike sent Steve back again.

By the time he returned, Pangpang was standing with Alex, looking perfectly ordinary.

Steve had found neither the equipment nor the underground bed, campfire, and chest.

He was still wondering whether that pit had done any good.

Pangpang brushed the last bit of dirt from his shoe.

He already knew the answer.
