_This time, he brought three blocks._

The Ender Dragon was defeated. Steve had the dragon egg, and he had collected elytra too.

Returning to the Overworld, he had good reason to consider the trip a successful completion.

Then he met a zombie.

Not another dragon. Not some powerful enemy hidden at the very end. Just a zombie.

It killed him.

He had barely finished collecting his elytra, and already he had a new question to deal with: how could it have been that thing?

Steve sat at the login screen and thought about it.

The more he thought, the less he liked it.

The dragon egg was real. The elytra were real. Losing to the zombie was also real. Lined up together, those three facts made him want to make an adjustment to the last one.

Steve switched characters and logged in again.

This time, he was not planning a prolonged contest with the zombie.

He brought three TNT blocks.

One went down. Another beside it. Then the third.

The opponent was still the same ordinary zombie. The level of preparation had changed completely.

Steve lit the TNT and stepped away.

After the explosions, he returned to look.

The zombie was gone.

There was not even any rotten flesh left.

Standing there, he realized he could not find a single item to pick up as proof that revenge had been achieved.

Three TNT blocks for one zombie.

Judged purely by the drops, the operation had produced no profit at all.

Steve did not log in a third time.
