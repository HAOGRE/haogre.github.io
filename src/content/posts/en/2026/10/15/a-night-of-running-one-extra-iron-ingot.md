Pangpang put on his iron boots and stamped twice.

They made a wonderful sound.

Nothing like yesterday, when he'd returned to spawn barefoot.

Steve put his iron sword away. At last, this new world seemed to be going right.

Then a chorus of groans came from outside the cave.

## Yesterday, They Only Got as Far as the Trees

When the three of them first spawned, Steve had a plan.

Wood first, then tools, then somewhere to live.

They completed a small part of the first step.

Then the zombies arrived.

The crafting table stood beside a tree. They hadn't even divided the wood. Pangpang turned and barely managed a few steps. Steve couldn't hold out either. Alex was trying to pull them clear when the screen went dark.

All three reappeared at spawn.

Steve looked up at the sky.

“Second start.”

Alex pointed back the way they'd come.

“First, collect the wood from the first one.”

They hurried to where they'd fallen and retrieved the items that hadn't despawned. This time, they didn't stand around the crafting table discussing things. They made tools and found shelter.

A wooden pickaxe got them stone. Stone pickaxes took them into the mine.

## This Time, There Really Was Enough Iron

Their luck underground was remarkably good.

They opened one iron vein and found more beside it. Around a bend, another cluster appeared.

Steve carried ore back, Alex kept digging, and Pangpang tended the furnaces at the entrance.

In the end, they had enough ingots for iron armor, iron pickaxes, and iron swords for all three, with materials left over as spares.

Helmets, chestplates, leggings, boots: one by one, the items came off the crafting table and were laid before their new owners.

Once dressed, Pangpang checked his feet.

Alex fastened her final piece of armor.

Steve held his iron sword and remembered yesterday's zombies. Suddenly, it seemed a shame.

If they met those again—

A groan came from outside.

## The Numbers Had Changed

Steve reached the entrance, saw the hillside, and swallowed the rest of his sentence.

The zombies wore no armor.

But there was a whole horde of them.

Zombies in front, zombies behind, and more emerging from the trees farther away.

Pangpang came to the opening and looked twice.

“We're wearing iron armor.”

“Yes.”

“They aren't.”

“Yes.”

“So—”

“Run!”

The three rushed down the side of the slope, around trees, and over a shallow ditch. Their packs rattled with every step. Every piece of their new equipment was still there, and none of it meant they could stop running.

Steve didn't look back to count.

Alex found the route ahead. Pangpang did his best to keep up.

At last they spotted a cave they could hide in. They squeezed inside and blocked the entrance with cobblestone, leaving only a narrow gap to check the sky.

Pangpang sat on the ground, gasping.

“The equipment got an upgrade.”

Steve leaned against the wall.

“Our legs didn't.”

## After Sunrise

They waited until dawn.

Out in the sunlight, the zombies caught fire one after another. Only when the sounds had stopped completely did Alex move the blocking stones.

This world had extra loot rules. Checking the drops outside, they actually found an iron ingot.

Just one.

Steve placed it on his palm.

Pangpang looked at it without reaching out.

“Yesterday, we mined all that iron.”

“Mm.”

“Today, we ran all that way. One extra ingot.”

“Mm.”

Alex checked both their packs.

Wood, tools, spare materials. Everything was there.

“At least we don't have to go back for our gear.”

Steve put the ingot away separately.

Pangpang used the wall to stand, then looked down at his iron boots again.

“These are all right.”

“What's all right?”

“Ran all night. They stayed on.”
