Pangpang fastened his chestplate and looked down at it for a long time.

“Now we look experienced.”

Alex straightened a crooked piece at his shoulder.

“No rush to prove it.”

Steve nodded. Fresh from their defeat in the city event, he was quite willing to agree.

## A Stack of Diamonds on the Road

As they returned to the main road, a player in diamond armor approached.

Steve instinctively stepped aside.

The player stopped, produced a stack of diamonds, and handed it over.

He didn't ask for anything in exchange or invite them to fight a monster.

It was simply a gift.

Pangpang looked at the diamonds, then at the departing player.

“Did he forget to take something?”

Alex shook her head.

“He said they're for us.”

They stood a moment longer before heading to the city's trading street.

Player merchants on this server bought all sorts of things at negotiated prices. Seeing the diamonds, several stallholders leaned over to offer equipment.

This time, Steve didn't browse the potions first.

They traded for three complete sets of unenchanted netherite armor.

Helmets, chestplates, leggings, boots. They put on each piece.

Pangpang lifted his arms, then inspected his legs.

Every place that had felt exposed now seemed protected by a plate.

Steve stood a little straighter too.

He still didn't suggest returning to the event.

## Back for a Piece of Bread

Their generous acquaintance appeared again.

He nodded at the new armor, then pointed to Steve's bread.

“Could I have a piece?”

Steve promptly handed one over.

The player accepted it and gave him an emerald in thanks.

Steve opened his mouth, emerald in hand. Before the words came out, the player was already heading down the street.

Pangpang leaned over to look.

“Gives away diamonds. Comes back to buy bread.”

Alex packed the remaining bread away.

“Next time, ask if he's hungry first.”

## A Result Before the Test

Noise broke out farther along the trading street.

Three players in full diamond armor pushed through the crowd, shoved a chest aside, and displayed their weapons.

The nearest merchants backed away.

The troublemakers were having an easy time.

Until they reached Steve and his friends.

The leader stopped, scanning Steve from helmet to boots, then looking at the other two.

Three sets of netherite armor.

Complete sets.

One companion leaned toward his ear. The three exchanged a glance. Their expressions changed at once.

They remembered the absurdly powerful player they'd encountered before.

He had worn armor like this.

Steve hadn't decided where to stand when the leader stepped backward.

Then all three turned and ran.

Pangpang watched them go and slowly lowered his arm.

“Did we win?”

Alex glanced at the weapons they hadn't touched.

“They left.”

Steve felt his chestplate.

“We haven't tested this yet.”

Pangpang considered that, then adjusted his helmet.

“Let's leave it untested today.”
