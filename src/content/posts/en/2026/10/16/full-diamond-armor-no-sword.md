The first time Steve reached for his belt, he was still running forward.

The second time, he slowed down.

The third time, he looked at Alex.

Alex was checking her belt too.

Pangpang held a golden apple in each hand. He thought they might be looking for an empty slot.

“I'm full too,” he said.

## Back to the Beginning

The three had just joined a new PvP server.

Steve hadn't even reached his first tree when dozens of players in diamond armor came charging toward them.

Alex didn't ask what they wanted.

Their swords were already raised.

The friends rushed into the trees, along a slope, and through a gap in the rocks. Footsteps followed them all the way. Only after several hundred blocks, tucked into a narrow cave entrance, did the noise finally fade.

Pangpang sat down and examined his empty hands.

“Fast progress in this world.”

“Whose progress?”

“Theirs.”

## Good Things Behind the Wall

Steve dug through a few blocks obstructing the cave. Firelight shone through from behind them.

It was another team's temporary supply camp.

Diamond armor filled a chest. Potions stood nearby, along with golden apples.

They hadn't asked the camp's owners. After such a miserable chase, they saw the supplies and stopped thinking much beyond them.

Steve pulled on a chestplate. Alex found leggings and boots. Pangpang collected apples. They crowded around the chest, filling their inventory slots row by row.

Their previously empty hands could barely spare a moment to close the lid.

Steve pointed toward the exit.

“Back we go.”

Alex looked down at her bright blue armor.

Pangpang took one more apple.

They charged out with considerably more confidence than they'd brought in.

## Face to Face at Last

The pursuers hadn't gone far.

Seeing the three return, they paused. Steve thought the pause was a good sign. He reached for his sword.

Only a potion at his belt.

He checked another slot.

Another potion.

Alex opened her hotbar and examined it from end to end. Pangpang put down one apple to free a hand.

An empty hand was still an empty hand.

“Where are the swords?” Steve asked.

“Didn't you take them?”

“I thought you did.”

Pangpang looked between them.

“I took these.”

He raised an apple.

The other players didn't wait for them to finish assigning jobs.

Soon all three were back at spawn. Armor, potions, and golden apples lay among their pursuers. They hadn't defeated a single opponent.

That trip from the supply camp had saved somebody else a walk.

## Now They Could Start

This time, they didn't charge into the crowd.

They kept running around the city's outskirts, leading the pursuers away. After looping through several thousand blocks, they slipped into town from another direction. They found a few supplies in a potion shop, then left the main road for a more distant wood.

Healing potions, regeneration potions, and strength potions.

Alex stowed them and looked at Steve.

He walked up to a tree.

“Wood first.”

Pangpang nodded.

“And then?”

Steve broke off the first log.

“Something we can actually hold and fight with.”
