The gatekeeper said new players couldn't enter.

Steve immediately opened his record.

“We've respawned three times.”

The gatekeeper checked it, glanced at Alex and Pangpang, then lowered his hand.

The gate opened.

As Pangpang walked through, he felt they'd finally found somewhere that respected experience.

## More Experience, Almost Immediately

This was a main-city event on their modded server. Its admission rules, challenges, and monsters were configured specifically for the event.

The three had just lost equipment outside and meant to restock in town. Seeing the crowd near the square, they'd stopped to ask about it.

Now that they had permission, naturally they wanted a look.

The gate shut behind them.

Mechanisms around the arena lit up one after another.

Steve looked left as an attack came from the right. Alex pulled Pangpang toward a wall. The wall offered no safe place either.

Soon the three were outside the city again.

On precisely the same patch of grass.

Pangpang brushed himself off and looked back at the gate.

“Even better qualified now.”

## Finally Past the Early Stages

They refused to give up.

On their second visit, they didn't all charge forward. Steve watched the mechanisms, Alex checked the sides, and Pangpang waited for the person ahead to find secure footing.

Pause, dodge, move a little farther.

At last, the place that had sent them outside lay behind them.

There were more stages ahead.

Wearing the equipment commonly used by experienced players in this event, they worked through the challenges one at a time. Eventually, they reached the final open arena.

Steve let out half a relieved breath.

The other half stayed in his throat.

A red-maned Lynel stood ahead.

It was a guardian added by the mod. The nearby description was clear: lowest level.

Pangpang read it and stood a little straighter.

The Lynel drew its sword.

Before they could decide who should go first, all three were outside again.

## The Equipment Was Correct

Steve read the equipment description once more.

Standard gear for experienced players.

They hadn't chosen the wrong set.

Alex pointed at the wording.

“It describes the equipment.”

“Yes.”

“It doesn't say wearing it makes us experienced.”

Pangpang said nothing. He was inspecting his soles, as though the stages they'd just crossed might still be stuck to them.

They tried again.

Sometimes they circled to the side. Sometimes they spread out. Whenever they finally got close, the Lynel turned and scattered their formation.

They tried very hard.

The monster didn't fall.

They didn't receive a reward.

## Walking Out This Time

On their final attempt, Alex backed toward the exit first.

Steve looked at her and put his weapon away.

Pangpang turned with them.

They didn't charge again or wait for the screen to darken. They walked through the exit, one step at a time.

The gatekeeper was still there.

Steve didn't raise his respawn record.

Pangpang crossed the threshold and glanced back to check.

“They didn't send us out this time.”

Alex nodded.

“We walked.”
