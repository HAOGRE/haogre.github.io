When Pangpang put the potion away, his secure storage had nine empty slots.

Afterward, it had none.

He left the interface open and withdrew his hand.

“That was one bottle, wasn't it?”

Steve counted the bottles.

Alex counted the slots.

Neither count was wrong.

## When They Gave Three Away

This PvP server had a special limit: at most one hundred strength potions in the entire world. Secure storage was another server feature. Items inside it wouldn't drop when a character died.

The three hadn't studied those rules when they first found strength potions.

They had just escaped their pursuers and started chopping wood when a player in netherite armor landed in front of them.

His gaze settled on their three bottles.

Steve compared their equipment with his and promptly handed over all three.

The player was delighted.

He didn't attack. He even left half a stack of golden carrots as thanks.

Pangpang stood holding the carrots for quite a while.

“Did we just make a deal?”

Alex watched the figure flying away.

“We can still chop trees, at least.”

## When They Bought One Back

Later, Steve remembered the potions at the old supply camp.

They hurried back to find empty shelves. After discovering the missing supplies, the owners had moved everything else.

The friends searched along the abandoned mineshaft on their way out. They found one diamond in a chest, then a regeneration potion in an inconspicuous wooden box near a wall.

This time, Steve opened his secure storage first.

The regeneration potion occupied one slot.

In the main city, they browsed the stalls. Eventually, they exchanged that potion and their three diamond swords for a single strength potion.

Three swords lay neatly on the counter.

The bottle they received wasn't large.

Pangpang carefully placed it in his secure storage.

All nine slots lit up.

Under the server's secure storage rules, this rare potion occupied exactly nine slots. The bottle looked no different. There simply wasn't any space left.

Steve stared at the interface.

“We gave away three before.”

Pangpang nodded.

“Now we've bought back one.”

Pangpang nodded again.

Alex closed the interface.

“Let's not hand this one over so quickly.”

## Different Slots, Different Jobs

Outside the city, they found some diamond equipment and a diamond pickaxe at an abandoned camp. They dug a small shelter into a ravine wall.

An old wooden chest sat in a corner.

Inside were three stacks of baked potatoes.

Steve placed them in his secure storage. Three slots used, six remaining.

Pangpang compared Steve's interface with his own.

“Yours holds quite a lot.”

“Yours is expensive.”

“Expensive isn't dinner.”

They sealed the entrance and finally slept.

## Couldn't Trade for It, Couldn't Loot It

The next day, a netherite-armored player stopped them and produced an invisibility potion.

He wanted the strength potion in Pangpang's storage.

The three refused.

He put his bottle away and raised his weapon. Soon Steve, Alex, and Pangpang were standing together at spawn again.

Their ordinary inventories were empty.

Pangpang opened his secure storage first.

The bottle was still there.

All nine slots were still occupied.

Elsewhere, the player searched their dropped items, failed to find the potion he wanted, and finally flew away without it.

Steve checked his own secure storage and handed each friend some baked potato.

Pangpang accepted his share, looking from the potion to the food.

“That bottle is good at keeping itself safe.”

Alex took a bite of potato.

“Let's try doing that ourselves next time.”
