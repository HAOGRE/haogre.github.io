This trial was Steve's alone.

No servant. No teammate following him to pick things up. This custom map had replaced “Main Character Luck” with “Main Character Misfortune.” At first, he thought the name was merely rude.

Then he tried to collect the dragon egg.

His pick came down. The egg moved. He went around to reach it, and it wasn't there anymore. The number of holes he had dug kept growing. The number of dragon eggs in his inventory stayed at zero.

Steve crouched beside a hole and looked at his pick.

“Run away again and I'm leaving you here.”

The egg said nothing.

He swung again.

## Delivery, With a Respawn Included

A shadow fell over him.

By the time Steve looked up, the chest was already close.

A moment later, he was back at his spawn point.

He felt his head, checked the sky, checked his surroundings, and finally spotted the chest. He opened the lid. The egg he had spent so long chasing lay inside.

Black, with purple markings.

The package was undamaged.

The recipient had needed another attempt.

Steve lifted the egg out and turned it over in his hands.

“So the luck is still there.”

That seemed worth celebrating. He did not rush to consider the rest of the arrangement.

The sky darkened again.

## Meals and Accommodation Included

This time, it wasn't a chest.

An obsidian enclosure, a hundred blocks by a hundred, dropped over the ground and shut Steve inside with his egg.

He went to the wall, tapped it, and searched all the way around.

The trial map would not let him mine through these walls. No entrance. No exit.

There was food, though.

Whenever he took some, more appeared. After a few attempts, Steve understood: the food supply was unlimited.

He sat down with the egg.

“Very considerate.”

Small objects began to clatter down from above.

Totems of undying landed at his feet. Then more followed. Steve shuffled aside to make room.

The trial's protection system would even use them on his behalf. Returning to spawn by losing his health had been accounted for, too.

He looked down at the egg.

“You've got a storage box.”

Then he looked around himself.

“So have I.”

## A View of the Outside

Steve did not give up immediately.

He searched slowly along the walls until he found a passage. Light shone at the far end. A breeze reached him from outside.

Holding the egg firmly, he hurried toward it.

Halfway through, something shifted overhead.

Several obsidian blocks fell.

The passage was still there.

It was just one block high now.

Steve stopped, crouched, and tried to edge forward. This map would not let him crawl through. His shoulders pressed against the stone. He was still inside, but sunlight had reached his boots.

He backed away and tried again.

No luck.

_The exit was real. So was the problem of getting through it._

Steve carried the egg back and sat where he had been before.

The food had replenished itself.

There were plenty of totems.

The egg rested quietly on his lap. At last, he no longer had to chase it with a pick.

He stared at the gap that admitted sunlight and, after a long while, spoke.

“The delivery has arrived.”

“Could you let the recipient out?”
