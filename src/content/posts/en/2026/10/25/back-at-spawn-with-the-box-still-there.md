A shulker box sat on the grass.

An elder guardian was beside it.

Steve watched from cover for a long time before he was certain: in this heavily customized Minecraft world, Flame really had left his box behind.

The player who handled a mace, a spear, and a trident so well had already gone toward spawn.

Pangpang looked from the box to Steve.

“He forgets things too?”

## Barely picked up, already caught

The three crept over and mined up the shulker box.

Steve had barely begun sorting things when he heard a rush of air behind him.

Flame was back.

The first mace strike sent Steve to spawn. A trident followed, and Alex went back too. Pangpang started to turn and run, but a wind charge and another mace strike reunited all three.

It had taken much less time than waiting for Flame to leave.

Pangpang sighed.

“We may have spent less time holding it than he spent coming back.”

Steve did not answer.

He was looking at his inventory.

## More than three things came back

The shulker box was still there.

Steve checked again before taking it out.

The netherite ingots inside were there too.

While adjusting the server settings, the owner had accidentally enabled Keep Inventory. Losing that fight had returned them to spawn without emptying their inventories.

Alex stepped closer to check.

Pangpang reached out and touched the box.

“He caught us.”

“Yes,” said Steve.

“And he won.”

“Yes.”

Pangpang withdrew his hand.

“Then why do we still have the box?”

## This time, he was after someone else

They had barely had time to enjoy the ingots when Flame appeared again.

All three ducked aside almost together.

But this time he did not turn. He charged straight toward another player farther ahead.

That player’s name was Wendu.

The two fought fiercely, with neither gaining a clear advantage. Holding the shulker box, Steve looked at them, then at the open path beside him.

Pangpang whispered,

“Wait until they finish?”

Alex was already leaving.

“No.”

The three carried the box away, walking quietly.

For once, they would make the return trip on foot themselves.
