_Plenty of treasure. No room for it._

Steve was pleased when he found the treasure beneath the lake.

Nine diamond blocks. And netherite armor.

He was wearing diamond armor at the time. Looking from one set to the other, it was difficult to argue that the swim had been a waste.

Then he opened his backpack.

No empty slots.

He checked his storage containers too. They were full of totems of undying. He had not used a single one. Usually, looking at them made him feel he had plenty of chances left. Now he did not even have a chance to put away a piece of clothing.

“Could you make room for something new?”

The totems did not.

Nor did the treasure become less valuable just because his inventory was full.

Steve looked at the new armor, then down at the diamond armor he was wearing. After a moment, he found a way around the crowded backpack.

If he could not pack it, he could wear it.

He changed into the netherite armor and moved his arms. At least this piece had somewhere to go now.

Then he examined the enchantments.

His first look had been too quick. He had thought there was Fire Protection. He checked again. There was not. One more look did not add it.

Part of his earlier excitement had come from something he had supplied himself.

Steve stood there for a moment. He did not take the new armor off.

“Then it doesn't have it.”

He decided not to add Fire Protection either.

He had now confirmed two things: he could wear the new armor, and his backpack was still full.

As for the nine diamond blocks, he looked at them, then at the backpack again.

Finding good things was clearly not today's problem.
