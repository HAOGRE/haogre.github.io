“Don't test it yet.”

Alex pushed the guidebook toward Steve.

Pangpang leaned in too.

They had fought through an entire temple for this blade. Now even a casual test seemed worth reconsidering.

## Read That Line Again

On this custom Minecraft server, Steve had acquired a Master Sword with a damage rating of six thousand.

High as that was, it wasn't the strongest weapon.

A hint had claimed that four temples contained elemental cores. A closer check revealed it was false. There were no cores and no final sword upgrade for collecting them.

There were trials and special rewards.

The three went anyway.

First stop: the Ice Temple.

## Three Unfriendly Levels

The first level's Golden Lynel demanded all the attention they had saved at the central temple.

The second held an Ice Gleeok.

This three-headed dragon was weaker than the King Gleeok they had met before, but it kept summoning ice pillars. Simply finding somewhere to stand was hard enough.

They dodged a wave, then struck in the brief gap. It took a long time to reach the next door.

At level three, thousands of ice-element Bobo charged them.

The little pig-like monsters ran on two legs, each carrying an ice sword.

When Pangpang saw the first one, its size seemed manageable.

Then he saw the line behind it.

After the last one fell, the three stopped at the door. Breathing first. Opening it afterward.

## Level Four Has Somewhere to Sit

Beyond the door lay grass and water, with no monsters.

A recovery area.

Pangpang tried sitting down. Nothing leaped at him, so he settled in.

Steve restored his status while Alex sorted their equipment. Only after a proper rest did they pick up their weapons and open the fifth door.

Inside stood a chest.

Inside the chest lay one Ancient Blade.

## One Blade, One Enemy, One Use

This server's special item could instantly remove a hostile creature.

Steve was considering how to use it when Alex spotted the restriction.

Each blade worked once. After defeating one enemy, it would vanish.

Pangpang checked the empty chest again.

No second blade.

For a moment, nobody spoke.

They had been thinking how convenient future encounters would be.

Now they were considering which monster would be worth it.

Steve put the Ancient Blade away and took up the Master Sword again.

“We'll use this one for now.”

At least this one had survived being used once.
