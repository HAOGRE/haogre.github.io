Steve had finally found a patch of grass he didn't have to distrust with every step.

He stood on one block, then moved onto the next.

Neither vanished.

“This place will do.”

Alex was still looking behind them. Pangpang crouched and touched the ground.

## Spawn, Then Run

They had just joined a new custom Minecraft server. Near the center of its world, terrain broke apart: blocks vanished and paths abruptly ended.

The three had barely formed a team when a crowd of gold-armored players charged at them.

Before Steve could choose a tree to chop, he had to choose a direction to run.

They jumped cracks and skirted collapsed slopes. Whenever the ground looked whole, they took a few more steps. Pangpang tried to count their pursuers. Alex pulled him forward.

“Count the blocks left in front of us first.”

They feared they were running deeper into the unstable center. Instead, the path grew more solid. Trees stopped floating, and slopes finally reached the bottoms of their hills.

The armored players' voices faded.

The ground stayed where it was.

## Somewhere to Put a Crafting Table

Steve walked around the clearing and announced they could start building up their supplies.

Alex checked the surroundings. No pursuers.

“Are we still running?” Pangpang asked.

“Not for now.”

Steve placed the crafting table. They gathered around to make tools, finally with time to arrange their inventories one item at a time.

They had even begun discussing which way their home should face.

Then a golden figure appeared across the grass.

This one had no player name.

The guidebook called it a Golden Lynel.

## No Need to Pick a Direction This Time

Before they had a proper grip on their new tools, the monster used one of this server's custom abilities: Planetary Devastation.

The grass, the table, and the proposed building site lurched together.

Then came the respawn screen.

When they could stand again, the landscape had changed. This spawn point was even farther away.

Pangpang looked at the ground, then at Steve.

“Is this one stable?”

Steve didn't answer immediately.

First, he checked all around them.

“Don't place the table yet.”
