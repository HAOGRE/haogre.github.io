Outside the temple, Pangpang asked a practical question.

“That fellow back there counted as the first level, right?”

Alex looked at the blue sword in Steve's hand.

“And the last.”

## The Map Goes Farther

On this custom Minecraft server, the guidebook could show buildings across the world as well as nearby monsters.

After checking their Lynel bow, sword, and shield, the three discovered they could expand the map.

A great temple stood at the center of the world.

Pangpang dragged the map toward himself. Even after considerable dragging, their position remained far from it.

“How long is that walk?”

Steve put the map away and started packing food.

It was a very long walk.

Across snow, through woods, over a river, and onward. They watched the path by day and checked the map whenever they stopped. Each resting place felt as though it ought to be close.

Then they checked again. Still farther.

When the temple finally came into view, Pangpang put his shield down and spent a while stretching his arms.

## A Familiar Gatekeeper

The first level held a lizard warrior.

They recognized this kind of monster. Working together, they defeated it and headed farther inside.

Steve saved his potions.

Alex counted her remaining arrows.

Pangpang tightened his grip on the shield.

The difficult part should be ahead.

At the end of the passage stood a staircase. They exchanged looks and climbed it. The stairs led to a teleportation circle. One flash later, they were at the top.

There was no second guardian.

Only a Master Sword.

## Six Thousand, No Enemy Beside It

The sword was blue, shining with blue light in its awakened state.

The guidebook showed its custom server damage rating: six thousand.

Steve drew it out, checked the number, then looked around.

No monster.

Alex inspected the walls. Pangpang watched the floor, waiting for something to emerge.

Nothing did.

This was the end.

They jumped into the return entrance and arrived beside the original vortex outside the temple.

That was when Pangpang asked his question.

Steve shifted the Master Sword to his other hand and felt the potions he'd saved for the final level.

The bottles were still packed safely away.

“If only we'd known,” Pangpang said.

“We'd still have had to walk all that way,” Alex said.

Pangpang checked the map and put his backpack on again.
