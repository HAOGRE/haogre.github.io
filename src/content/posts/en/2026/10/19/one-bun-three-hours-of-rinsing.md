Steve spent three hours beside the water.

He did walk away occasionally.

After a few steps, he came back.

Eventually Alex brought him a stool. Steve looked at it, shook his head, and reached for more water.

Pangpang stayed in the kitchen. He didn't call Steve to eat again.

## Before the Lid Came Off

In this village world with its cooking mod, Pangpang had recently made burgers.

Everyone had enjoyed them.

He decided steamed buns were worth trying too.

Steve asked if he needed help. Pangpang said to wait; they'd be ready soon. Alex wiped the table. Passing villagers heard there was something new and stopped for a look.

Everything went smoothly while the lid was on.

When the steam cleared, everyone shifted backward a little.

A few misshapen lumps leaned together on the plate. The color was wrong. So was the shape. One side had collapsed, and another lump clung to it. It was hard to tell where the dough ended and the filling began.

Steve studied them for a long time.

“Are these finished, or haven't you started yet?”

Pangpang pushed the plate toward the center of the table.

“Finished.”

## Only One Hand Reached Out

The villagers took none.

Neither did Alex.

Pangpang began, “Maybe it's only how they look.” Then he looked down again and stopped.

Steve didn't want his friend to keep standing there with the plate.

He chose one that still vaguely resembled a bun.

Pangpang immediately straightened up.

Before Alex could speak, Steve had taken a bite.

The room went quiet.

Steve's expression changed.

He turned away, spat out the mouthful, set the bun on an empty plate, and headed straight for the sink.

Pangpang examined the missing bite.

“Not good?”

The sound of water answered for Steve.

## From One Cup to Three Hours

At first, everyone thought a quick rinse would do.

Steve thought so too.

He put down the cup, took a few steps toward the door, stopped, and came back.

A second cup.

After a while, he tried to say something. Opening his mouth made him grimace again.

More water.

Alex covered the failed buns so later arrivals wouldn't take one by mistake. Pangpang carried the plate back to the kitchen and inspected the steamer again.

This time, he didn't say it might only be their appearance.

Time passed slowly.

The villagers left. The table was wiped. Alex even finished bringing over the stool.

Steve still returned to rinse his mouth now and then.

Only after three full hours did he finally set the cup down without picking it up again.

## No Second Bun Today

Pangpang stood at the door and quietly asked whether Steve would help him taste something next time.

Steve didn't answer immediately.

First he looked toward the plate.

It had been taken away.

Then he checked the cup.

It was still within reach.

“You eat it first next time.”

Pangpang nodded.

“Finish it before you call me.”

Pangpang nodded again.

Alex left the stool beside the sink.
