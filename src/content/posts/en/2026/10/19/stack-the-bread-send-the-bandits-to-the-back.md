Steve carried plates into the kitchen and found Pangpang putting meat on bread.

Nothing unusual there.

But greens sat on the meat, sauce beside the greens, and another half of the bread on top.

Steve stacked his serving plates again.

“These don't go separately?”

Pangpang carefully supported the assembled thing.

“Let's try keeping them together.”

## The Meals They'd Received First

This was a different new world, with cooking and village interaction mods.

Soon after spawning, the three found a village. The villagers gave them bread, a place to stay, and help getting tools.

A few days later, Steve knew the path to the wheat fields. Alex had recorded everything they'd borrowed. Pangpang remembered most clearly who had fed them.

He wanted to return the invitation.

They didn't own anything very expensive.

Pangpang helped harvest wheat and borrowed the village's cooking station. Bread, patties, greens, sauce: he prepared each ingredient, intending to serve them separately.

The worktop quickly filled up.

While moving things around, he set a patty on a split roll, added greens, and replaced the upper half.

He lifted it and examined it.

Perhaps it didn't need a plate.

## Surprise Begins at the Door

Steve received the first one.

He inspected the side, then the top.

“Where do I start?”

Alex had already taken a bite of the second.

“The edge.”

Pangpang waited nervously.

Alex didn't put hers down. After trying his, Steve didn't hand it back either.

That reassured Pangpang faster than praise.

By the time the villagers arrived, a row stood on the table.

The villager who'd given them bread picked one up. He recognized the bread, studied what was inside, and looked up at Pangpang several times.

Pangpang said it was called a burger.

The villager tasted it, then turned to call the people outside.

More people soon appeared outside.

Word reached the main city. Passing players started taking the turn into the village. Some only meant to look. After standing a while, they asked how many were left.

Steve's job changed from carrying plates to moving tables outdoors.

Alex reminded everyone to leave the entrance clear.

## First Trouble, Then the Queue

Three diamond-armored players stood outside the village.

They'd intended to take advantage of the crowd and rob somebody.

As they approached, their leader stopped to look at the table. His companions followed his gaze and stopped too.

Villagers held burgers. So did visitors from the city.

Steve stood beside the table, eating his.

The leader put his weapon away.

He moved toward the crowd and tried to edge forward.

Alex pointed.

“Back there.”

He paused, then turned and noticed the queue.

All three diamond-armored players walked to its end.

## The Same Rule for Seconds

When their turn came, the three traded for burgers.

Their leader took a bite and forgot whatever he'd been about to say.

Pangpang waited.

“Well?”

The player took another bite before nodding.

Today's robbery never happened. The three stood aside with their burgers while the queue moved forward.

After finishing, the leader leaned toward the table again.

“Can I trade for another?”

Pangpang looked at Alex.

Alex pointed once more.

The queue was longer now.

The player inspected his empty hands and walked to the back. His companions followed and took their places.

Steve brought out another tray of burgers and glanced toward the end of the line.

Those three weapons had stayed put away.
