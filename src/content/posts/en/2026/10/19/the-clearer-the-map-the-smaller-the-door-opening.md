Pangpang reached the cave entrance and immediately returned to the cave.

He hadn't walked back.

Setting the respawn point nearby had saved the journey home.

## Start with the Weakest Entry

On this custom Minecraft server, monsters from another world had been added to the landscape. A Golden Lynel had already sent Steve's team to respawn once.

The next day, they spotted something similar.

This one was red.

They checked the server guidebook before approaching. Only after confirming that the Red-Maned Lynel was the weakest kind did they try fighting it.

This time they won.

It dropped three items: a Lynel Bow, a one-handed sword, and a shield.

Under this server's rules, the bow used one arrow to fire three, with an attack rating of thirty. The sword also had thirty attack; the shield had fifty defense.

Steve took the sword, Alex the bow, and Pangpang the shield.

At last, their legs weren't their entire defense.

## Another Use for the Guidebook

Alex found a map page that marked nearby monsters.

As soon as she noticed an elite marker close by, she called the others underground.

Outside stood a slender lizard warrior, less powerful than a Golden Lynel.

Steve relaxed a little at “less powerful.”

After a brief attempt outside, he hurried back in.

The entry hadn't said they could beat it.

Later, they ventured to the entrance again. Steve and Alex ducked out of sight. Pangpang was a moment slower.

An enormous figure loomed over him.

Next moment, he stood at the snow cave's respawn point, his hand still held out as though peeking around the doorway.

Alex checked the guidebook.

Hinox. Two legs. One eye. Extremely strong. Very slow.

Pangpang waited until she finished.

“Slow still caught me.”

## Almost Three Days

They reduced their range after that.

They organized supplies inside and watched from the entrance. If the map showed danger, they waited a little longer.

Nobody volunteered to declare the place safe.

Almost three days passed this way, without another powerful monster arriving at their door.

Pangpang sat eating. Alex checked the map and closed it. Steve finished repairing a tool and looked up to find both friends still in their seats.

“Good day today.”

Pangpang nodded.

This time, he had made the trip from the entrance to his chair on his own legs.
