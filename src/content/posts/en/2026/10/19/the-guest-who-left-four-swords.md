Three knocks at the door.

The visitor wore ordinary clothes. His name was Flame, and he asked for a place to stay.

Steve welcomed him in, already calculating how much room they could clear.

The visitor inspected the house first.

## Newcomers Look Alike

This was Steve, Alex, and Pangpang's new home on a Minecraft server with special mechanisms.

They kept their valuables well hidden. Only everyday supplies stood in view.

Flame looked like a newcomer too.

At least, that was what everyone thought until he produced four diamond swords.

Laid side by side, they brightened the table considerably.

Pangpang counted them.

“There are three of us.”

Flame didn't take the fourth back.

He turned out to be a formidable player investigating a bounty worth three netherite blocks. His inspection had found no problem. Instead, he left these items behind.

Before Steve could decide what to ask next, Flame unfolded his elytra and flew away.

They hadn't even cleared a place for him to sleep.

## More People at the Door

The next day, dozens of diamond-armored players arrived to cause trouble.

These visitors didn't knock or ask for lodging.

The three stayed inside. Their defenses moved first.

Before the leading attackers reached the house, the mechanisms sent them to respawn. Those behind looked at the suddenly empty path and ran.

Alex waited until nobody was charging before switching off the defenses.

Pangpang glanced at the table.

The four swords were still there.

## No Request for Lodging This Time

Flame returned.

This time he put half a stack of diamonds on the table as thanks.

The bandits had troubled the other players for almost a year. Loaded with instant-damage potions, they were difficult to clear out. Today, they had run into this house's defenses.

Steve looked at the diamonds, then at the door.

“We didn't even go outside.”

“I know.”

A long string of fireworks suddenly burst overhead.

The three stepped to the doorway and watched colored blocks of light scatter across the sky.

Pangpang looked back at the table.

Yesterday they had needed room for a guest. Today they needed room for the gifts.

Steve moved a stool aside.

Best leave the entrance clear, in case the next visitor needed somewhere to stand.
