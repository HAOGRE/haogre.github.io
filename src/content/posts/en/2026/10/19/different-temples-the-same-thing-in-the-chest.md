Pangpang recounted the two trials to Steve while looking at an Ancient Blade.

When he reached the Lightning Temple's third level, he stopped for water.

The blade still looked just as short.

## Four Levels of Little Monsters

After the Fire Temple, the trio continued through the otherworldly temples on this custom Minecraft server.

They prepared carefully for the Shadow Temple.

Level one: low-level monsters.

Level two wasn't difficult either.

After level three, the fourth contained only a few more little monsters.

Steve suspected the place was trying to lower their guard. At level five, he discovered their carefully maintained guard had never been needed.

The chest held one Ancient Blade.

Pangpang would keep it.

They checked the earlier blades too: Steve had the Ice Temple's, the three-player team that had helped them had the Fire Temple's, and Pangpang now had this one.

None had been used.

## The Next Temple Had Other Ideas

Level one of the Lightning Temple held King Gleeok.

Three heads waited behind the door.

Pangpang swallowed his unfinished suggestion that this temple might be easy too.

Level two held an ordinary Golden Lynel.

“Ordinary” was relative to what followed.

At level three, three Golden Lynels came at once: one fire, one ice, and one poison.

Dodge the fire, go around the ice, find a clear spot—and watch poison drift into it.

Steve told Pangpang not to back up. Only after looking behind him did Pangpang see why.

They kept changing positions and covering one another until the final guardian fell.

Nobody called it easy this time.

## Rest at Level Four

Level four was another recovery area.

Pangpang put down his shield and sat faster than he had accepted the Shadow Temple's reward.

They restored their status and went to level five.

Another chest.

Another Ancient Blade.

Steve spent a while reading the description.

Still single-use.

“Same thing as the Shadow Temple?” Pangpang asked.

“Yes.”

“We didn't open the wrong chest?”

Alex put away the new blade and showed him the temple's name on the map.

Right chest.

Pangpang stayed seated a little longer.

He wasn't waiting for an explanation. His legs hadn't recovered yet.
