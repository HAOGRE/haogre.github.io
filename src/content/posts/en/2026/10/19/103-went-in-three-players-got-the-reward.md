A hundred and three players stood outside the temple, diamond armor shining in a broad crowd.

Pangpang looked behind him. The back of the party was still forming up.

“Enough people this time?”

Level one showed them that counting people outside and fighting monsters inside were different things.

## Half Gone at the First Level

This was the trio's next temple trial on their custom Minecraft server.

The Ice Temple had been hard enough with three. Hearing that the Fire Temple was worse, they returned to the city to recruit help.

Including themselves: 103 players.

Level one held another Golden Lynel.

The party filled the arena. When the monster charged, the front ranks retreated while those behind were still pressing forward.

Steve shouted for space. Alex searched for a clear shot between people. Pangpang turned with his shield and nearly collided with a teammate.

By the time they won, more than fifty players had been sent to respawn, ending their runs.

The back of the party no longer needed to wait outside.

## Better Armor, a Shorter List

Steve handed out twenty sets of enchanted netherite armor to the core fighters.

Level two: a Flame Gleeok.

The three-headed dragon swept the arena with fire. Diamond-armored players dropped out one after another. Only the core group in netherite could keep going.

The enormous guardian at level three occupied half the arena.

They circled it and took turns attacking through openings. By the time it fell, the party was more exhausted still.

Level four held the Lava Regent.

Flames forced the remaining players backward. Steve had the Master Sword in his hands, but nowhere firm to stand.

Then three of the experienced fighters charged together.

They belonged to one team and used this server's in-game self-destruct attack. TNT flashes swept the arena. The Lava Regent fell, and the three attackers were sent to respawn too.

An empty battlefield remained.

## One Blade in the Chest

At last, Steve's trio reached and opened the final chest.

Another Ancient Blade.

Another single use.

Pangpang looked at the blade, then at Alex.

They didn't divide it.

Back in the city, they found the three fighters' team and handed over the one blade.

The other three didn't divide it either.

They were already a team. It could go into their shared supplies.

A hundred and three had entered, and the final chest was small.

Steve didn't search it again for a second blade.

They knew where this one belonged.
