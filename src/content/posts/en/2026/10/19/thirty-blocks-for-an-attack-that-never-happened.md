Steve was still holding a firework when the goat horn sounded.

Thousands of diamond-armored players were moving toward the main city along the distant road.

Pangpang put away the one he hadn't launched.

That horn was clearly not part of the celebration.

## A Different Sound Between the Fireworks

This custom Minecraft server allowed battles between servers. The city had barely driven off a bandit group when an outside force arrived.

Defenders gathered at the gate. Flame went to meet the attack.

The trio didn't linger at the street corner. They went back to check their valuables.

After the attack passed, they secured their three netherite armor sets, golden apples, and several diamond swords in hiding again. They returned to the city carrying only three ordinary diamond swords.

Fireworks were rising over the square once more.

Pangpang found the unused one in his inventory. At last, a moment to launch it.

## Leather Armor This Time

They thought it was peaceful again.

Then several thousand more players came from the other server, wearing leather armor and carrying stone swords. They meant to push in from behind through sheer numbers.

Flame spotted them first.

He placed dozens of in-game TNT blocks, sending the crowded leading ranks to respawn, then sounded his goat horn.

The city's players rallied. The rest of the attack failed to advance.

Steve's team still carried their ordinary diamond swords. The netherite stayed hidden. They were becoming very familiar with the horn.

## Thirty Blocks Beside a Name

The other side refused to accept defeat. They put Flame's name on the bounty board.

Thirty netherite blocks.

A highly skilled assassin accepted the job.

This time there was no marching crowd, no gleaming mass of armor. The assassin approached quietly, planning to strike before Flame could react.

Flame reacted first.

Before the assassin could land a single attack, he was defeated and sent to respawn.

The thirty-block bounty went unclaimed.

The other side finally stopped coming.

Pangpang waited. Then waited a little longer. No new horn.

“Can we finish this time?”

He meant the fireworks.

Steve checked the city gate before passing him a light.

The sky brightened.

This time, no horn interrupted it.
