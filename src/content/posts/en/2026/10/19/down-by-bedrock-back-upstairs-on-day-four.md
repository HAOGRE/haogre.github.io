The map filled with red dots.

Alex zoomed in, then out.

She wasn't mistaken.

More than a hundred lizard warriors stood outside.

## That Many Ordinary Monsters

Steve's team lived on a custom Minecraft server that spawned monsters from another world. Their snow cave had been quiet for a few days. Now the entrance was busy again.

These weren't elite markers.

At “ordinary,” Pangpang almost relaxed. One look through the vent stopped him.

Numbers made up for rank.

They gripped their Lynel bow, sword, and shield. Before they could agree on a plan, something blotted out the light.

King Gleeok had arrived.

Three heads leaned toward the cave.

The three never got a chance to strike back. They were at the respawn point instead.

## Nothing to Live in Upstairs

When King Gleeok left, the surface fell briefly quiet.

Steve was about to investigate when three dangerous markers appeared on the map.

Three Golden Lynels.

This time they didn't even leave the door standing.

The server's Planetary Devastation ability tore up snow and stone. As the roof came down, the three were already running deeper into the mine.

Pangpang started asking what to bring. One glance back answered him: himself, first.

They retreated to the ground just above bedrock and sealed the entrance.

No snow-house window here. No vent to look through.

Just three people and a torch.

## Day Four

On the first day, they listened to the sounds above.

On the second, they listened again.

On the third, they heard nothing. They looked at one another and still didn't hurry to open the entrance.

Only on day four did they climb back up.

The monsters were gone from outside the snow house.

They recovered the scattered and buried belongings a little at a time. Their Lynel sword, bow, and shield were all there. They set them within reach and counted them twice.

Holding a bundle of recovered supplies, Pangpang stood beneath the damaged roof.

“Are we staying here?”

Steve looked toward the passage down into the mine.

“Keep the stairs clear first.”

This time, when they repaired the house, they left that route open.
