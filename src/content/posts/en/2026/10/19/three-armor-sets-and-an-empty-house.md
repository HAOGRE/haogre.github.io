The chest was empty.

Pangpang opened the second.

Empty too.

Steve closed the door, walked around the room, and decided they'd done well.

## Sent Back to Spawn First

This was another new Minecraft world, on a server with custom mechanisms.

Steve, Alex, and Pangpang had barely spawned in the main city before netherite-armored players sent them straight back to respawn.

Pangpang was a little easier to spot than the other two.

As he crouched over some blocks outside town, Steve told him to get lower.

“This is as low as I go.”

The server had a special button rule: even blocks that normally stayed in place could fall when triggered.

They used it to set a trap and lured the three players underneath. Button pressed, blocks fell. Their pursuers were buried and eventually became dropped equipment.

Three sets of netherite gear lay on the ground.

The trio collected them faster than they'd discussed the plan.

## Back into the Least Noticeable Clothes

They dug a little cave and packed the armor, weapons, and potions into chests.

Strength, fire resistance, speed: each potion went away in its place.

They left the valuables there and sealed the entrance with stone.

Back in town, all three looked newly spawned again.

After a few steps, Pangpang glanced at the hillside.

Steve reminded him that a real newcomer wouldn't know anything was hidden there.

They traded the gems they'd picked up for supplies. At last, they could shop normally.

The three original attackers later left the server. Following some trading and a handover, Steve's team took over their empty house.

## Empty Can Still Be Useful

That was where the two chests came from.

No secret treasure. No reward bursting out when opened.

Pangpang shut the last lid.

“They were thorough.”

Alex checked the window, which closed properly. Steve put his things beside the table.

Before, every equipment check meant another trip behind the hill. They worried about being seen on the way in and on the way out.

Now they had a visible home. At least they wouldn't have to visit the same hidden opening every day.

Pangpang began filling a chest with what they'd brought.

Food first. Ordinary tools next.

Steve watched the room begin to look lived in and opened the door again.

This time, going outside didn't require moving a stone block first.
