_The armor was back where it started. There were still two templates._

Steve set out in fully enchanted netherite armor with full durability.

In this modded save, elder guardians could drop smithing templates. He meant to collect a few.

After fighting more than twenty guardians, he counted his haul.

Two templates.

He checked the slots again in case he had missed something. Still two.

Then he checked his armor. Half its durability was gone.

“Not many templates. The armor certainly got some use.”

Steve decided on a change of location. He headed underground to look for resources he wanted and repair his armor with Mending along the way. At least mining and fighting guardians were different activities.

His pickaxe struck stone for a while. The things he wanted did not appear.

He kept going. His backpack still contained no new treasure worth taking out to admire.

The armor, however, was gradually repairing itself.

When Steve stopped, its durability was full again. He stood in the tunnel and counted the day's gains once more.

Smithing templates: two.

Desired resources: none.

Armor: full durability.

That last item looked quite good. The trouble was that it had looked that way when he left home.

He touched his chestplate, then looked at the two templates.

“Wear half of it out, then make another trip to fix it.”

By that measure, he had certainly done plenty today.

What he had to show for it was easier to count.
