The first time Steve ran out of the deep dark, he was clutching an ender pearl.

When he set off, he had two totems of undying.

Now he had one.

Alex stood at the edge of their temporary camp, looked at him, then looked toward the tunnel.

“How is it in there?”

“You can get in,” Steve said.

Pangpang waited.

Steve added the rest. “Getting back is the important part.”

## A Rather Loud Cup of Tea

Steve decided to visit the warden for tea again.

He prepared a strength potion for himself and a poison potion for his host. As he left, he felt he had arranged the refreshments quite sensibly.

Before long, a muffled boom came from the cave.

Steve's health dropped to a sliver. He hurriedly used a wind charge to get some distance, threw an ender pearl, and appeared near camp again.

Pangpang looked at him.

“Finished your tea?”

“It filled me up with a sonic boom.”

Steve sat for a while, then went down once more.

An ender pearl brought him back this time, too.

All three stared toward the tunnel. Once the cave went quiet, it somehow looked even less inviting.

Steve finally admitted it. “I can't beat it. I really can't.”

Alex was about to nod when he turned to fetch his shulker box.

## This Time, Bring the Bed

Inside were the strength potions Steve had been saving since the server opened.

An entire box. He normally hated wasting them.

This time he brought the box, carried over a bed, and set his spawn point in a safe spot.

Pangpang looked at the bed.

“So you're ready now?”

“The return trip is ready.”

After that, Steve went in and Steve came out. Potions went in; empty bottles came back. Sometimes Steve arrived at the bed first and worked out everything else afterward.

More and more slots in the box were empty.

The warden was still there.

Steve crouched beside the box and shifted the remaining bottles. Most of what he had spent so long saving was already gone.

“I didn't just brew this lot,” he said.

“We can tell,” Alex said.

“Been saving it since the server opened.”

Pangpang glanced toward the tunnel, then back at the box.

Over there, something was still standing. Over here, quite a lot was missing.

## The Big One Was Done. Then Came the Small One.

Later, Steve found three more strength potions.

He put those three to use as well and went into the deep dark again.

This time, the warden finally fell.

Steve stood still for a moment.

He looked ahead, then checked his own health, making sure he hadn't ended up back at the bed again.

He had won.

There were things on the ground, waiting for him to pick them up.

Steve had just stepped forward when a bowstring sounded in the dark.

A moment later, he was back beside the bed.

The empty bottle in Pangpang's hand stopped in midair.

Alex asked, “The warden again?”

“No.”

“You haven't finished it?”

“I have.”

“Then what was it?”

Steve looked at his empty hands.

“A skeleton.”

There was more.

The things Steve hadn't had time to collect had been picked up by the skeleton.

One of his two totems was gone. Most of a box of potions was spent. He had even brought a bed. At last, the warden had been beaten.

The one who collected the loot had brought no potions and moved no bed.

It had only brought a bow.
