The first thing Steve saw after spawning was someone getting banned.

The second was Pangpang dragging him into a half-dug hole.

Alex crouched at the entrance and blocked out the sky with the last block of dirt.

“Now what?” Pangpang asked.

Steve looked at his empty hands.

“We don't even have a door yet. Let's make a door.”

## People Start Falling into the Basement

They had just joined a modded test server. Their inventories were full of unfamiliar gray icons. They hadn't figured out the server rules yet, but the ban notices in chat were hard to miss.

The three of them decided to live underground for now.

Alex dug the tunnels, Steve tended the furnace, and Pangpang farmed and handed out bread. Gradually, the little hole acquired a crafting table, chests, and a row of torches. Once his iron boots were ready, Pangpang took a couple of steps across the stone slabs. Much louder than yesterday.

“Can we go outside now?”

First, a block of dirt fell from the ceiling.

Then a person.

Diamond armor hit the floor with a clang. Pangpang hurriedly scooped up the bread chest and moved it aside.

Alex raised her sword. Steve raised his torch. The stranger looked up, and a name emerged through the dust.

Ender Hunter.

The one who'd been banned when Steve spawned.

“Weren't you banned?” Steve asked.

“The ban glitched.” Ender Hunter sat up. “I'm still in here. The admin's looking for me.”

He set three diamonds on the crafting table. He said he'd caught the admin hoarding test gear and ended up on the ban list after questioning him about it.

The three exchanged glances.

Pangpang handed him some bread first.

“Take your time. Can you use the door next time?”

Ender Hunter nodded.

Before long, there was another noise overhead.

This time it was a player in a full set of enchanted netherite armor, named Deep Valley Miner. He had barely climbed out of the dirt when a whole crowd came down after him.

Everyone had a problem with the admin.

Nobody had much interest in the door.

Steve had to keep digging, widening the shelter on both sides. They added a row of furnaces, organized the chests, and stored the diamonds they mined while everyone took turns crafting gear. The little hole that had barely held three people now had a queue for the anvil.

Pangpang put up a sign beneath the ceiling.

> This is not the entrance.

After a moment's thought, he moved the bread chest a little farther away.

## Netherite Couldn't Stop That Hit

When the group finally geared up and went to find the admin, the basement fell quiet.

Pangpang walked in the middle, feeling very safe this time.

Netherite armor in front. Netherite armor behind.

But the thing the admin pulled out wasn't part of any equipment set they knew.

It was an Ocean Trident, a test item from the server that he'd illegally modified to ban players. As its blue light swept over them, players vanished from the group, their names appearing in chat at the same moment.

Nobody got to finish what they'd come to say.

“Run!” Steve shouted.

Everyone heard him this time. But only five made it into a side tunnel: Steve, Alex, Pangpang, Ender Hunter, and Deep Valley Miner.

Pangpang leaned against the wall and counted them. Then counted again.

“Stop counting,” Alex said.

He stopped and broke the bread in his hands into five pieces.

## Twenty Thousand Points—for Whom?

Deeper in the rock, they found a purple-black mineral.

Steve mined it with his netherite pickaxe. Exactly two pieces. The crafting table recognized a test recipe, and the sword it produced was called an Inkcrystal Sword.

Base damage: 300.

It also had an ability that let its wielder fly.

Pangpang scrolled to the bottom of the stats panel to make sure he hadn't missed any zeros.

Once they'd opened up the vein, all five made themselves swords and put together sets of Inkcrystal Armor. A full set added 20,000 to their maximum health.

“The sword does the damage. The armor adds the health,” Alex reminded them.

Pangpang nodded. “I've got that very clear now.”

He took a short test flight in a safe side tunnel and knocked over an empty chest on landing. The chest went down. He was fine.

“An improvement,” said Deep Valley Miner.

Pangpang set the chest upright. “Didn't fall into anyone's house this time.”

The five regrouped and headed out along the narrow tunnel. Steve was already thinking about how to get past the trident. Pangpang was wondering how long 20,000 HP would last.

At the front of the line, Ender Hunter suddenly stopped.

Pangpang ran into his back. Steve ran into Pangpang. Alex lost her footing, and Deep Valley Miner barely got out half a “Wait.”

All four ended up in a heap.

“Sorry,” said Ender Hunter.

Pangpang looked up from the floor. “You should've said that sooner.”

Ender Hunter opened the test item list again.

“I remembered wrong.”

He pointed to the slot at the very top.

“Inkcrystal isn't the only new gear here.”

The other four waited for him to finish.

“It's the weakest tier in this batch of test gear.”

The tunnel went quiet.

Pangpang looked down at his reassuringly long health bar.

The bar hadn't changed.

The reassurance was gone.

Alex put her sword away. “Then let's head back. We still haven't figured out the gear, let alone the bans.”

Steve nodded.

The five turned around and walked back to their original little basement. Once inside, Pangpang checked the ceiling to make sure nobody else was falling through it today.

He turned the sign over and wrote on the back.

> Even with 20,000 HP, knock first.
