When Pangpang first saw the giant on the fifth level, its head wasn't the first thing he noticed.

It was a patch of gold on its shoulder.

The gold was moving.

“That's a lot of gold,” he said.

The Deep Valley Miner squinted at it for a moment.

“That's a lot of monsters.”

A moment later, they both knew the miner was right.

## The Spawn Point Was Close

This modded dungeon let them respawn over and over. Steve and the others had already faced a diamond-headed giant on the first level, but this one on the fifth was bigger, and the golden monsters crawling over it were fiercer.

One jumped off the giant's arm.

Pangpang raised his shield.

The shield took one hit.

All five of them were soon back together at the spawn point.

Pangpang still had his shield up. Only when he noticed the torch beside him did he slowly lower it.

“I didn't even get a good look at it.”

“Let's go look again,” Steve said.

On their second try, they decided to split up.

The monsters split up too.

On the third, the Ender Hunter climbed onto a rock to shoot from higher ground. The little golden things scaled the rock face and reached his feet before he could fire.

There was the torch at the spawn point again.

Alex looked up.

“This place is getting pretty familiar.”

Pangpang turned his shield over to inspect it. “Familiar enough that I want to put a chest here.”

## Forget the Giant for Now

Steve sketched a route on the ground.

From the stone pillar by their feet to the giant's knee, then up along a crack on the outer side.

“We keep trying to rush straight to its head,” he said. “But every time we run, all the monsters behind us come after us.”

The Deep Valley Miner added a few blocks beside the route.

“One section at a time. Clear a section, then move our cover forward.”

This time, Pangpang didn't charge ahead. He held the narrow gap between two rocks while Steve and Alex took turns moving up. The Ender Hunter waited for the monsters to come into the open before shooting. The Deep Valley Miner watched their flank, replacing blocks wherever one was missing.

They still got sent back.

But each time, they made it a little farther first.

Two blocks at the beginning.

Eventually, as far as the knee.

Pangpang had lost count of how many times he'd died. All he knew was that they had less and less bread, and the giant's face was still a long way off.

“Has it gotten taller?”

Alex glanced up.

“No. We've fallen back again.”

## Three Hours to Clear a Path

After an hour, they finally had somewhere to stand their ground.

It took about two hours to reach the giant's chest.

Golden monsters squeezed out of cracks and crowded onto the rock face. Just when the five of them thought the numbers were thinning, scratching started up behind them again.

The Ender Hunter reached into his quiver and paused.

“Not much left.”

Pangpang looked at his shield. “Same here.”

The Deep Valley Miner handed him a spare.

That shield wasn't new either.

Pangpang took it without complaint.

Steve spotted a narrow opening in the giant's chest and pointed out the last stretch of their route. Alex cleared the left side first. Pangpang held the right. The Ender Hunter saved his arrows for monsters jumping down from above. The miner rebuilt the missing steps behind them so anyone sent back to spawn would still have a way to catch up.

They fought over this stretch for a long time.

Long enough that Pangpang stopped talking.

When the last golden monster dissolved into specks of light, all five stood outside the narrow opening. No one went in right away.

Steve checked the time.

Almost three hours.

Pangpang sat down against the rock wall. “Now we fight the giant?”

No one answered.

High above them, the giant's head remained still, gazing into the distance.

## It Was Bright Inside

They went through their backpacks.

Supplies were low, and their equipment was badly worn.

Alex gave everyone a little of what was left, keeping the last potion for herself. The Deep Valley Miner caught up from below and took his place at the back.

“Everyone's here.”

Sword in hand, Steve stepped into the dark opening in the giant's chest first.

He was ready to dodge the first attack.

Instead, the ground beneath his feet suddenly lit up.

The darkness ahead gave way to a clean wall. Wooden chests lined it, with more chests behind them. Warm lights glowed overhead, and the floor was flat enough to set down a bed.

There was a label on the wall.

Level Six.

Pangpang followed, his old shield still raised. He kept it there for a long time.

“Where's the giant?”

Steve looked back. The passage was still there, but no giant fist came through after them.

Alex opened a chest and found potions and spare equipment.

The Ender Hunter opened another and pulled out a bundle of arrows.

The Deep Valley Miner ran his fingers over the lettering on the wall.

It really did say six.

“So we spent all that time fighting,” Pangpang said, “just to walk through it?”

Steve put his sword away. “Looks that way.”

Pangpang looked down at the notch in his shield.

He put the old shield in an empty chest, picked out a new one, then took a spare as well.

“Next time I see a giant, I'm asking it first.”

“Asking what?”

“Are you a boss or a hallway?”
