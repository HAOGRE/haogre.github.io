The throne on the seventh floor stood very high.

So high that Pangpang spent a while looking up at it after he walked in, before remembering to raise his shield.

The Demon King sat on it.

Steve stopped. “We're only on the seventh floor, and we're here already?”

Alex finished reading the label.

“A double.”

Pangpang let out a breath.

Five assistants stepped out in front of the throne.

He sucked it straight back in.

## Get in Line

They were working their way through a dungeon built for testing mods. The creatures in its stages came from different settings, and their abilities had been altered. Having fought one before didn't necessarily mean knowing its next move.

The five assistants blocked every approach to the throne.

Steve had barely stepped forward when one came around from the side. Pangpang blocked it for him, only for another attack to fly in from behind. The Ender Hunter found a good position, but the healer in the back restored the front assistant's health.

“How much damage have we done?” Pangpang asked.

Alex watched the health bar fill back up.

“Which time?”

They had to divide up the work again.

The Deep Valley Miner used blocks to close off the side routes. Pangpang took the front, while Steve and Alex looked for openings. The Ender Hunter kept watching the back line until the healer was exposed.

This time, the health bar finally stayed down.

Once all five assistants had dissolved into points of light, the Demon King's double slowly rose.

Drew his sword.

Lifted his head.

Prepared to speak.

Hoofbeats from the side got there first.

## Who Was the Boss Again?

A golden Lynel charged into the arena.

Pangpang only managed “There's another—” before the impact knocked his shield aside.

The five scattered. Every position they'd planned for fighting the Demon King fell apart. The golden Lynel charged to the far end of the arena, then turned back. Wherever its blade swept, nobody wanted to linger for even one more block.

Steve blocked a hit and lost a chunk of his health bar.

Alex tried to circle behind it. It turned faster than she could.

The Deep Valley Miner had barely placed his blocks before discovering that the little extra height did no good.

The Demon King's double stood in the distance, sword still in hand.

Nobody had time for him just yet.

“Which one do we fight first?” the Ender Hunter shouted.

Pangpang answered on the run. “The one fighting us!”

The fight dragged on. They drank plenty of potions, but never found a chance to deal damage safely.

Then, just before an attack landed, Steve happened to dodge to the side at exactly the right moment.

Everything around him slowed.

Scattered stones hung in the air. The golden Lynel's blade moved as though it were caught in thick water. Steve had time to turn back and land several hits in a row.

By the time everything returned to normal speed, he was safely out of reach.

Alex had seen it.

“Don't try to block it. Wait for it to attack, then dodge.”

In this test stage, a perfectly timed dodge triggered a brief window of bullet time.

It took one sentence to explain. Putting it into practice still meant getting hit.

Pangpang dodged too early on his first try. The Lynel turned with him.

On his second, he dodged too late and went looking for another potion.

On his third, the blade brushed past the edge of his shield, and the whole arena finally slowed down.

“Now!”

All five seized the brief opening, then immediately scattered. Wait for the next attack. Dodge again. Land a few more hits.

At last, the golden Lynel's health bar began to shrink, little by little.

After its final charge, it didn't get back up.

## One Arrow Becomes Five

Among the loot were exactly five Savage Lynel Bows.

One each.

The Ender Hunter took out an arrow and drew his new bow to try it. The arrow split into five in flight, spreading across the distant stone wall.

Pangpang looked down at the bow in his hands.

“One arrow goes in?”

“Yes.”

“Five come out?”

“Yes.”

Pangpang put his old bow away with particular care.

“Now this level's starting to make sense.”

Someone coughed behind them.

All five turned.

The Demon King's double took two steps forward.

Steve gripped his sword again. Alex circled to the side. Pangpang raised his shield, and the other two moved farther away.

They brought all the tension of the last fight with them.

They didn't end up needing much of it.

The double's health bar dropped far faster than expected. Before the five could even switch to a second strategy, the dark spectral figure dissolved, leaving only a few pieces of ordinary gear in front of the throne.

Pangpang stood where he was, looking from the throne to the spot where the golden Lynel had disappeared.

“So the real Demon King was off to the side.”

Steve gathered up the drops. “The one up there had the title.”

“The one down here had the muscle.”

Alex pointed toward the open exit.

“Let's go.”

Pangpang took one last look at the throne.

“The chair was his, at least.”
