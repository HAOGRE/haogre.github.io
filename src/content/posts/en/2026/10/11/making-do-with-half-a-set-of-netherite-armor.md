The first thing Steve did when he sat up at spawn was take back what he'd just said.

He'd said, “There's only one of him.”

As it turned out, one was enough.

Alex opened her eyes in the next bed. Pangpang returned a little later and stood in the doorway, opening his empty inventory for them to see.

“Anyone still want to ask him why he's attacking us?”

No one raised a hand.

## First, Learn the Three Armor Trims

The server allowed players to fight each other and had custom weapons installed. The other group's gear all looked much the same: netherite armor. You had to get close to tell them apart.

The one who came most often wore redstone trim. He charged fast, and his mace hit hard.

The player beside him wore black trim. He didn't stand out from a distance, but everyone else in the group waited for him to move first.

Then there was a player with amethyst trim. He rarely spoke and never seemed to be in a hurry. The first time he showed up, Steve thought they'd finally found someone they could handle.

After that, Steve stopped judging opponents by how fast they walked.

Their village took a beating. They retreated to the next one, only to be caught again soon after. Eventually, the three of them hid in a cabin in the mountains, shut the door, and even kept their voices down.

Pangpang slid the bar across the door.

“Maybe he won't find us here?”

A moment later, something struck outside.

Pangpang still had the bar in his hands. The door had already broken apart.

The three of them retreated through the passage at the back. The figure in purple paused at the fork. He didn't take the wrong turn or rush blindly ahead.

Alex glanced back.

“He remembers the way.”

## Half a Set Is Half a Set

Once they'd shaken him off, they went to the Nether.

This time, they weren't looking for a fight. Steve mined, Alex kept track of the route, and Pangpang counted their supplies. They laid out the ancient debris they'd brought back and worked out how much upgrade material they could make.

Not enough for a full set each.

Pangpang suggested making one set of the best armor first.

Alex looked at him. “For who?”

Pangpang said nothing more.

In the end, they each upgraded their chestplate and leggings, keeping their diamond helmets and boots. The colors didn't quite match, and nobody had time to think about trim. But all three could take another hit now.

Steve turned around in his new armor.

“Want to try again now?”

Alex flipped over the materials list. On the back was a tally of how many times they'd respawned.

Steve sat down.

“Let's come up with a plan first.”

They surveyed the mountain pass and found a narrow stretch anyone coming through would have to use. They repaired an old defensive mechanism, cleared the escape route, and used blocks on their side to mark the area that would trigger it.

Pangpang asked if they could make the markings bigger.

“We need to remember,” Alex said. “They don't.”

## Five Sets of Armor in the Pit

Five lackeys came to ambush them.

All five wore full netherite armor. The moment they spotted Steve, they gave chase. Steve backed along the route he'd practiced, Alex watched the fork, and Pangpang hid behind a stone wall, waiting for the last player to enter the pass.

When the mechanism went off, even he jumped.

The path collapsed in a cloud of explosion particles. By the time the smoke cleared, all five were back at their own spawn points. Their gear and a few golden apples lay in the pit.

The three of them didn't jump down right away.

First they checked the hillside, then the entrance on the other side, making sure there wasn't a second team.

Only then did Pangpang start picking things up.

“Five sets,” he said.

This time, it really was five sets.

They equipped the pieces with the most durability left and divided up the rest for safekeeping. Steve looked at his full set of armor and, for once, didn't immediately suggest a counterattack.

Pangpang did it for him.

“Think we're about ready now?”

## He Was Still Waiting Below

The player with amethyst trim was waiting at the foot of the mountain.

He only fought them for a short while.

First, Steve saw his durability bar plunge. Then he heard a piece of armor break. Alex pulled him back behind a rock. Pangpang dropped the spare weapon he'd been reaching for and blocked the path first.

By the time the three of them had steadied themselves, their opponent had already turned and walked away.

Three pieces of armor gone.

They still had the gear they'd won. The gap was still there, too.

Steve laid the remaining gear on the ground and checked its durability, piece by piece.

“Should we chase him?” Pangpang asked.

Steve looked up at him.

Pangpang answered his own question. “Just asking.”

Alex crossed out the road at the foot of the mountain on their map and drew another route that would take them home the long way around. Today's loot was enough to repair and regroup. There was no need to lose it all in a second fight.

Back at their door, Pangpang put the spare armor in a chest and noticed that two pieces were still their old diamond gear.

“Keep these too?”

“Keep them,” Steve said.

Alex closed the chest and stuck a small note on the lid.

> Check durability before heading out.

Pangpang added another.

> If you see purple, check your escape route first.
