_Full durability. The same four opponents._

Checking his equipment, Steve felt well prepared.

A full set of enchanted netherite armor. A spear, ender pearls, water, and bandages. This was his modded save, and his inventory also held plenty of netherite weapons.

Ahead were four wardens.

His plan was straightforward.

Fight them head-on.

He tried dropping from above and striking one with his mace. In his plan, he would bounce upward next, ready for another blow.

The mace landed.

He did not bounce as expected.

With four wardens nearby, that difference mattered.

Later, he kept returning. One more try, then another. Whenever he checked his equipment, the armor was still called netherite armor. Losing had not renamed the enchantments either.

The results did not improve to match the impressive equipment list.

Twenty-five deaths.

When Steve said the number aloud, he added, “A full set of netherite.”

As though someone might have missed that important condition.

His armor had lost half its durability. He went to fight other mobs and gradually earned the experience needed to mend it. Those battles finally produced a visible change: the durability bars began to fill.

When they were full, Steve stopped.

The armor was fixed.

He still had not beaten the four wardens.

He looked at his chestplate and thought about those twenty-five attempts. Putting the two facts together did not make a victory.

What he had repaired today was the equipment.

The plan to win by fighting head-on was still exactly where he had left it.
