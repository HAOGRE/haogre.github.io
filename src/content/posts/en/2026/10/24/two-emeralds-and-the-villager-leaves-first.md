_The trident was ready. Someone else left first._

The obsidian pit in this custom world had kept Alex at the bottom for quite a while.

There was water in the pit and Steve outside it. He clearly had no intention of helping her out.

A zombie fell in, turned into a drowned, and eventually left behind a Riptide trident under the world's peculiar drop rules.

Alex picked it up. At last, the bottom of the pit seemed to contain an exit.

Using the water, she activated Riptide and shot upward.

Steve stopped her at the rim. She fell back down. The trident stayed above.

Looking up, she saw him copy the weapon and give another one to Pangpang beside him. This world allowed equipment to be copied. It did not guarantee that the equipment would reach whoever needed it.

After a while, Pangpang dropped a trident into the pit.

Alex caught it and checked immediately.

No Riptide.

Three points and a handle. Useful for poking things. Useless for leaving this pit.

She held it and stared up at the people above.

Then a villager fell in.

He gave her two emeralds as well. Alex quickly obtained a Riptide enchantment book and applied it to the ordinary trident in her hands.

This time, she checked carefully.

It had Riptide.

The water was at her feet. She tightened her grip, ready for her second attempt to fly out.

The villager reached over and snatched the trident.

A moment later, water swirled upward and he shot out of the pit.

Alex remained where she was, looking up.

The trident certainly worked.

Someone had just verified that for her.
