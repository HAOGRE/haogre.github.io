Pangpang had barely made out the sky when Steve nudged him toward the ground.

“Look later. Dig.”

Alex had already dug a hiding place three blocks deep and sealed the top. Three new players squeezed into the dirt while hunters walked overhead.

This was a new custom Minecraft world. Supplies could fall from the sky, pursuers could appear on the road, and a peaceful start was not included.

## The chest arrived first

A heavy thump sounded above them.

One set of footsteps stopped.

They waited for silence before clearing the dirt. A wooden chest sat on the grass. The hunter who had stood there had been sent back to spawn.

Pangpang looked at the sky, then at the chest.

“Should we put on helmets before opening this?”

Conveniently, it contained helmets.

Three complete sets of diamond armor: helmets, chestplates, leggings, and boots. A moment earlier, Steve had been wondering where to mine iron. Now he was fastening a diamond chestplate over his teal shirt.

Pangpang flexed his arms and pointed toward the hunters’ camp.

“Now we can go.”

## The first warning

They never made it through the gate.

A dark figure dropped from above, followed by a mace. The player wore netherite armor. In this world, with its modified combat rules, his mace had a particular way of arguing with armor durability.

One exchange later, all three diamond sets were broken.

The player lowered his mace.

“Just a warning.”

His elytra opened, and he flew away.

Pangpang looked down at his sleeves, visible again.

“Did we spend too much time getting dressed beside that chest?”

Alex pulled him back down the path.

They had not entered the camp. Their mining plans, however, had returned.

## Plenty of supplies, the same approach

Before they could gather enough iron and diamonds, a thousand players in diamond armor came charging toward them.

They charged in formation. They fell into the deep pit in formation too.

The three friends had dug that pit earlier. When the last sounds faded, heaps of diamonds and armor lay at the bottom.

Now the problem was carrying it all.

Pangpang changed into a fresh set and checked a spare helmet.

At the mouth of the ravine, the familiar netherite armor appeared again.

All three raised their weapons and charged.

Their opponent threw a wind charge at his feet. A burst of white air launched him upward, and the mace came down. Steve was still turning toward the landing spot when another wind charge burst.

They never caught him. Their diamond armor did not survive either.

The player landed and said the same thing.

“Just a warning.”

Then he opened his elytra and left.

The three stood still for a while. Pangpang hugged his spare helmet to his chest. This time, he did not put it on immediately.

“Going again?”

Alex turned toward their storage.

“Let’s finish listening first.”
