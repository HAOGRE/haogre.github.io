The hollow tree was small, just large enough for Steve, Alex, and Pangpang.

Pangpang tried to turn around. The other two held him still.

“No remodeling yet.”

This was their new custom world. They had finally gathered some equipment, but their old opponent in netherite armor had come after them with a mace and sent them running again.

By the end of the chase, the tree hollow had become their temporary home.

## A different weapon outside

The sounds outside stopped.

Steve had barely poked his head out when a spear swept toward him. A large part of his health bar vanished.

Their opponent had not left. He was waiting above them.

The three ran out, circled back, and squeezed into the same hollow again.

This world had more weapons and aerial moves than they were used to. Leaning against the hollow’s wall, Steve kept thinking about that strike.

“He learns too fast. I haven’t figured out the mace, and he’s already mastered the spear.”

Pangpang shifted sideways.

“At least we’ve mastered getting into this tree.”

## No advantage at spawn either

When they emerged again, they changed locations. Near spawn, they prepared to wait for their opponent.

The trident arrived first.

Alex was sent back. Steve and Pangpang did not last either, and soon all three had met up at the respawn point again.

Their opponent flew away after the fight without taking the equipment on the ground.

His inventory was packed with potions.

Pangpang looked toward the departing player, then at the dropped items.

“Finally. Something he’s bad at.”

“What?”

“Sorting his inventory.”

They still had no answer to his fighting, but they had identified a problem with his storage.

## At last, a name

A different route did not help.

An explosion sounded behind them. The player had used his bow to set off a TNT minecart, sending all three back to spawn once more.

This time, Steve temporarily ran out of complaints.

A mace, a spear, a trident, a bow, and a minecart.

“Is this all the same person?”

“Same armor,” said Alex.

Later, Steve finally read the name above that player’s head.

Flame.

He turned and ran, calling the others back to the place where they had first hidden from the hunters. They dug down, filled the top, and sat in the dirt again.

“Who exactly is Flame?” Steve whispered.

Alex answered slowly this time.

“The strongest combat expert on this server.”

Pangpang looked up at the dirt they had just put back.

“Then let’s practice this today.”

“Practice what?”

Pangpang withdrew the hand he had been reaching toward the opening.

“Keeping our heads down.”
