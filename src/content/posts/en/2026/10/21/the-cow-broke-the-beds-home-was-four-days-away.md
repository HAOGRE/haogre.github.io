The second time Steve returned home from spawn, he didn't ask what there was to eat.

He checked the beds first.

They looked exactly as they had four days ago.

Or rather, the few planks that remained of them did.

## They Paid for the House in Armor

The three had started another modded world. Its village offered special housing trades, and animal collisions worked differently from vanilla Minecraft.

When Steve, Alex, and Pangpang first arrived, they all had iron armor but nowhere to live.

A villager pointed at an empty house, then at what they were wearing.

The three took off their armor and laid it together. The villager inspected the pile before finally nodding.

It was just enough to get them the house.

Once inside, Pangpang touched the wall.

“Now the house can protect us.”

Steve thought that made perfect sense.

Later, he learned that it helped to be inside it first.

## The First Trip Was Steve's Alone

Steve never quite worked out which movement had upset the iron golem.

By the time he wanted to find out, he was back at spawn.

Instead of the walls of their new home, he saw the grass from their first moments in this world. The village was a long way off. Too far to shout.

He had to run back.

He traveled by day, hid for a while when darkness fell, then kept going. After three days and three nights, Steve finally recognized the rooftops.

He leaned on the doorframe as he came in. Alex and Pangpang were eating bread.

Steve looked at the table.

“I've spent the last few days looking for this door.”

Pangpang shifted over.

“You found it. Sit down.”

As Steve sat, trading their armor for a house seemed worthwhile after all.

At least he didn't have to chase the chair.

## The Second Time, the Beds Went Too

Later, two beds became Steve and Alex's respawn points in the village.

At last, they could get some sleep.

Then a cow came in.

In this world, a collision with that cow did more than shove a player aside. It stepped onto the bed frames, breaking the planks, then trampled the two players before they could get out of the way.

The game screen went dark.

Steve found himself at the distant spawn point again. This time, Alex was beside him.

He looked at her, then around them.

“I've walked this route.”

“How long?” Alex asked.

“Three days and three nights last time.”

This time, it took four days.

They dodged mobs and took detours. Whenever rooftops appeared in the distance, they had to stop and work out whether it was their village. On the fourth day, they finally dragged themselves home.

Pangpang stood inside, holding a little iron nail a villager had given him earlier. It was a small item from this modded world. Today, he was using it as a prop for a joke.

“A cow sent you all the way back.”

Steve said nothing.

“And broke your beds.”

Alex said nothing either.

Pangpang raised the nail like a very small sword.

“I've still got this.”

## This Time, the Golem Didn't Swing

While they listened, the iron golem arrived at the door.

Steve immediately stepped aside.

This time, the golem sent nobody away. It opened its hand, giving Steve and Alex an iron nail each, then gestured toward Pangpang.

They looked at the little items in their hands.

Then at Pangpang.

He had turned back toward the table, apparently unaware that they now had props of their own.

Steve approached first, hoping to give him a little poke.

He missed.

Alex reached out next and didn't touch him either.

Pangpang turned around and lightly tapped Steve's hand with his own nail.

Steve pulled his hand back at once.

That was the only move Pangpang made.

The golem still stood at the door.

Steve looked at it. “Whose side are you on?”

Pangpang lowered his nail.

“At least this time you don't have to walk for four days.”

Alex put her nail on the table and nudged Steve.

“Close the door first.”

Steve went over to it.

This time, he checked for cows first.
