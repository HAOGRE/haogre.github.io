The first sky feather led to a palace beneath the sea.

Steve, Alex and Pangpang spent two days preparing water-breathing potions. They spent another half day explaining why Pangpang had placed bread inside his helmet.

“Bread gives you courage in an emergency,” Pangpang said.

Alex looked at him. “Bread absorbs water. It gives you weight.”

Pangpang kept it there. A real adventurer carried emergency food.

## Day Fifty: The Ocean Tyrant

The sea suddenly exploded.

An enormous ocean tyrant rose from the depths. Eight tentacles surrounded its body, each one holding a blue gem. The waves shook even the distant islands.

The dragon amulet displayed:

> Boss: Ocean Tyrant
>
> Special mechanic: Tidal Shield
>
> Destroy all eight blue gems before attacking the boss.

Alex would shoot the gems. Steve would fight up front. Pangpang would attract the tentacles.

“Leave it to me!” Pangpang said.

A tentacle slammed down.

Pangpang ran.

“I thought you were attracting it!” Steve shouted.

Pangpang sprinted around a palm tree. “I successfully attracted it!”

Alex destroyed the first gem. Steve jumped onto a tentacle and struck the second. Pangpang kept running, with all eight tentacles forming a confused line behind him.

Then a coconut fell from the tree.

Thunk.

It hit the tyrant on the head.

“See?” Pangpang said. “My strategy worked.”

When the eighth gem broke, the sea split open. Steve charged through the gap, Alex fired from above, and Pangpang fell out of the sky holding a treasure chest. The chest landed on the boss.

It was not planned. It worked anyway.

The chest contained diamonds, emeralds, gold, an enchanted book and a blue orb called the Heart of the Sea.

The second sky feather rose from the chest and revealed a staircase leading upward through the water.

“I am officially an underwater warrior,” Pangpang announced.

Alex removed the soggy bread from his helmet. “Start by not storing food in your helmet.”

## Upgrading the Base

They carried the Heart of the Sea and the treasure home. Pangpang poured the diamonds onto the floor and was immediately buried by his own wealth.

“Help! I’m being crushed by fortune!”

From day fifty-two to day fifty-nine, they built farms, a warehouse, a smithy, watchtowers, a small dock and a room for the dragon egg that they still had not found.

Steve checked the empty room every day.

“Maybe it overslept,” Pangpang said.

“Maybe it isn’t ready,” Steve replied.

Alex pointed at the amulet. “Then we find the rest of the feathers first.”

## Day Sixty: The Sky Islands

On day sixty, a huge floating island appeared overhead. A golden beam shot from the ground into the clouds and pulled all three adventurers into the sky.

Pangpang looked down.

“Oh no.”

He grabbed Steve’s leg.

“Nobody pushed you,” Steve said.

“I’m defending myself in advance.”

The islands were covered with cloud roads, sky sheep and strange creatures. A small sheep bumped Pangpang.

“It wants to push me off!”

The sheep bumped him again, then rubbed against his side.

“It wants to play,” Alex said.

Pangpang patted its head. “Its communication style is aggressive.”

## The Talking Cloud Maze

The third feather was hidden inside a moving cloud.

Once inside the maze, they discovered that the clouds could talk.

“Turn left,” said one cloud.

“Don’t listen to it,” said another. “Yesterday it sent ten people into a dead end.”

“Even the navigation clouds get lost?” Pangpang asked.

Steve remembered the paths, Alex watched the shadows, and Pangpang questioned every suspicious cloud. They eventually found a rainbow bird carrying a golden feather.

The bird landed in front of Steve and placed the feather in his hand. The amulet flashed with the same blue light that had surrounded the dragon egg.

It was the third sky feather.

## The Storm Cloud and the Golden-Feather Eagle King

The fourth feather was inside a storm cloud.

Alex searched for its signal. Steve blocked the lightning. Pangpang hugged a floating stone.

“I am definitely losing weight today!” he shouted.

A gust pushed him sideways across the island.

“The weight loss has failed!”

Inside the storm, they met sky sprites protecting a cracked island core. Steve held the crack open, Pangpang carried cloudstone, Alex searched for the feather, and the sprites stitched the break with cloud ribbons.

After the repair, the sprites gave them the fourth feather.

Cloud deer then led them across a crystal bridge into the guardian’s territory.

On day seventy, a huge golden eagle burst from the clouds. The amulet displayed:

> Boss: Golden-Feather Eagle King
>
> Special mechanic: Wind Barrier
>
> Break four wind runes.

Alex shattered the first rune. Steve held back the storm. Pangpang threw his returning axe and shouted, “Super Pangpang Spin-Axe!”

The axe missed the boss and cut down a tree. Coconuts fell everywhere. One hit the eagle king.

“I told you my strategy worked!” Pangpang said.

The second and third runes broke. The eagle summoned a giant tornado. Steve braced behind his shield while Pangpang clung to the crystal bridge.

Alex fired the final arrow.

The fourth rune shattered. The wind barrier disappeared, but the eagle king did not attack again. It recognized their courage and dropped a huge golden feather.

The amulet read:

> Sky feathers: 4/10

The eagle king left them with one warning:

> The real enemy has not appeared yet.

Far above them, the Cloudheart Palace waited. The cracks in the dragon egg were growing deeper.
