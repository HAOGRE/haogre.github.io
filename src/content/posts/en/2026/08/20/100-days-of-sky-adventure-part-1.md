When Steve, Alex and Pangpang woke up, there was no ground beneath them.

They were standing on a newly generated sky island. It was tiny: one tree, a few blocks of dirt, and a chicken pecking seriously at empty air. Clouds stretched in every direction, while distant islands looked as if someone had scattered blocks across the sky.

Pangpang looked at the void, then at the only piece of bread in his backpack.

“Do you think our base will stay up this time?”

“Only if you don’t mine the last block of dirt,” said Steve.

Alex was studying a blue stone tablet in the center of the island. A blue amulet rested on it, covered in letters none of them could read.

When Alex lifted it, a sentence appeared:

> The real trial has not begun.

Pangpang divided the bread into three pieces, keeping the smallest one for himself because he was in charge of carrying supplies. Alex did not argue. She simply divided his piece in half again.

They did not know what the message meant. They only knew that every new world began with cutting trees, building shelter and waiting for a creeper to perform an unscheduled structural inspection.

## Day One: Survive First

Steve cut wood, Alex mined stone, and Pangpang went looking for food.

Half a day later, Steve had made a stone sword and pickaxe. Alex had organized the tools. Pangpang finally returned with one chicken in his hands.

“A feast!” he announced.

Steve stared at the chicken. “That’s one chicken.”

“The important part of a feast is the meal, not the size.”

They all laughed. The chicken escaped while they were laughing, and Pangpang chased it for ten steps before slamming on the brakes at the edge of the island.

That was their first meal, and their first reminder that the island was dangerously small. One careless step could make a lost item impossible to recover.

## Day Three: A Creeper Inspects the House

They spent three days widening the island.

Steve built the walls, Alex organized the storage and farms, and Pangpang carried materials. He carried them very seriously, although he ran too fast and fell into a hole he had just dug.

“I’m inspecting the trap!” he shouted from the bottom.

On the fourth night, a creeper climbed across the cloud bridge. It studied the sign at the entrance:

> Monsters not allowed, especially creepers.

“If you can read, you know you’re not welcome,” Pangpang said.

“It may not be able to read,” Steve said.

“Then it should at least understand the picture.”

The creeper hissed. Pangpang turned and ran. The explosion removed one wall, one of Pangpang’s shoes, and nothing from the sign.

Alex turned the sign around and wrote: “A sign cannot stop a creeper. Leave an exit.”

By day ten, they had a warehouse, farms, furnaces and a defensive wall. Pangpang labeled the chests “important,” “very important,” and “Pangpang do not open.”

The next day, the last chest was empty.

## The Amulet Beneath the Mine

To expand the base, they entered the mine beneath the island.

They found iron, coal and redstone. Pangpang was carrying the torches when a creeper appeared behind him.

“Steve, I found a green—”

Boom.

Pangpang flew out of the cave. Steve and Alex found him hanging from a branch, his hair standing straight up.

“Are you alive?” Steve asked.

Pangpang touched his face. “Alive. My hairstyle is not.”

At the deepest point of the mine, they found a second fragment of the amulet. When it joined the first, blue light filled the tunnel.

> The frost dragon sleeps inside a crack in the sky. Ten sky feathers can wake it.

“So we didn’t arrive here by accident,” Alex said.

Steve closed his hand around the amulet. “Someone was waiting for us to find it.”

Pangpang held the amulet at arm’s length. “I only want to know whether the frost dragon eats people.”

## The Volcano and the First Blue Eyes

From day eleven to day thirty, they followed the blue line through forests, deserts and a glowing ice field.

Some forest creatures feared fire. Others feared the amulet. A few ran away as soon as they saw Steve.

“Why do they fear you?” Pangpang asked.

“Maybe I look like a boss,” Steve said.

Inside an ancient ruin, they found a map of a dragon. In one corner it said:

> After the frost dragon is defeated, it leaves a reward that can change the sky.

The map led them to a huge volcano. Black smoke rose from the crater. Lava froze into violet glass halfway down the slope.

The amulet trembled in Steve’s hand.

Two enormous icy-blue eyes opened inside the smoke.

Pangpang raised his shield. “That does not look like a dragon that wants to be woken.”

The eyes vanished. A column of frost shot into the sky and froze the clouds into a slanted staircase. A feather drifted down beside the amulet.

It was the first sky feather.

Pangpang put it at the very back of his backpack and checked it every few steps. On the way home, it began to glow. A new marker appeared on the map, far out in the middle of the sea.

Steve looked toward the water.

Alex folded the map. “The second feather is underwater.”

Pangpang tightened his grip on the shield. “The volcano was not so bad after all.”

He had no idea what was waiting beneath the waves.
