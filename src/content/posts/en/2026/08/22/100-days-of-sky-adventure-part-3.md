After the Golden-Feather Eagle King left, the sky became quiet again.

Cloud whales moved beneath the islands. Cloud deer drank beside the crystal bridge. Sky sprites protected the feathers one by one.

The cracks in the dragon egg grew deeper.

“Crack.”

Pangpang jumped up. “Is it coming out?”

The egg did nothing.

“Maybe it just snapped its fingers,” he said.

## Day Eighty: The Sky Trial

The eagle king returned, not to fight, but to train them.

Steve learned to control the wind. He had to stand inside a storm and keep his shield facing the eye. On his first attempt, the wind blew him across three islands and onto the roof of Pangpang’s newly repaired warehouse.

Alex received a storm bow. Its string was made from thundercloud, and its arrows could turn with the wind. She learned to shoot through clouds without being struck by lightning.

Pangpang received a returning axe.

He threw it around a mountain and through two clouds. It came back and hit his helmet.

“It has a direct way of recognizing its owner,” Alex said.

The dragon egg absorbed the power of the sky. Its blue markings grew brighter.

> Frost dragon hatchling is growing.

## Days Eighty-One to Eighty-Nine: Before the War

On day eighty-one, they repaired the sky base.

On day eighty-two, they found the seventh sky feather in the Cloudheart Palace.

On day eighty-three, Pangpang discovered a secret warehouse filled with chests marked “do not open.” Every chest contained chicken bones from his private collection.

On day eighty-four, part of the island collapsed. They repaired the core with the sky sprites and saw black cracks beneath the clouds for the first time.

On day eighty-five, a black floating fortress appeared in the distance.

On day eighty-six, shadow creatures invaded the sky islands.

On day eighty-seven, the dragon egg had more cracks than anyone could count.

On day eighty-eight, Steve received sky armor. It allowed short bursts of flight. Pangpang put it on and immediately crashed into the warehouse.

On day eighty-nine, they prepared for the final battle, dividing potions, arrows and food into three packs.

Pangpang prepared an extra bag of bread.

“Is that enough?” Alex asked.

Pangpang patted the bag. “It is strategic equipment.”

## Day Ninety: The Egg Hatches

The sky turned black.

Thunderclouds covered the islands. The black fortress opened its gates. The Shadow Storm Lord stood above it with a storm spear, six dark wings covering half the sky.

The amulet displayed:

> Boss: Shadow Storm Lord

The battle began.

In the first phase, the boss summoned shadow copies. Steve held the front line, Alex fired continuously, and Pangpang swung his returning axe.

“None of you are getting away!”

The axe hit one shadow and returned into Pangpang’s nose.

In the second phase, lightning struck across the islands and broke the cloud bridges.

In the third phase, the storm core appeared in the center of the fortress. A black tornado closed in from every direction.

Just as they were about to be overwhelmed—

Crack.

The dragon egg split open behind Steve.

A tiny ice-blue dragon crawled out. The first thing it saw was Steve.

The amulet changed:

> Frost dragon hatchling
>
> Partner: Steve

Steve smiled. “You are our partner now.”

The dragon leapt into his arms.

Then it sneezed.

Frost covered Pangpang’s face. His eyebrows and beard froze at once.

“Why am I always the first target?”

Everyone laughed. Even the dragon sneezed again, more softly this time.

## Training the Frost Dragon

From day ninety-one to day ninety-four, the young dragon trained with them.

It learned frost breath, an ice shield, danger sense, treasure finding and how to freeze small monsters.

It could not fly very high, but it kept practicing beside Steve. On its first attempt, it hit a cloud. On its second, it hit Pangpang. On its third, it landed safely on Steve’s shoulder.

It was no longer a reward inside an egg. It was their partner.

## Day Ninety-Five: The Tenth Feather

They completed the final sky trial and found the tenth feather.

All ten feathers fused above the Cloudheart Palace, forming a great flying cloak. When it opened, the whole sky lit up.

Now they could fly freely—and call every friend to the same battlefield.

## Day Ninety-Six: The Sky Army

The sky sprites joined first.

Cloud whales rose from the clouds to block the cracks below the islands. Cloud deer brought the crystal bridges. Rainbow birds carried supplies. The Golden-Feather Eagle King stood on the highest island and spread its golden wings.

Pangpang stared at the army. “We have an army now!”

“You are in charge of supplies,” Alex reminded him.

Pangpang looked at his bread bag. “Supplies are the most important part.”

## Days Ninety-Seven to Ninety-Nine: The Last Defense

The black fortress released an army of shadows. Steve led the charge, Alex attacked from above, Pangpang swung his returning axe, and the frost dragon froze enemies in the air.

The first line fell quickly.

At the final sky wall, Pangpang raised his axe. “Who dares come closer?”

A wave of monsters charged.

“I can handle this alone—”

Ten seconds later, he shouted, “Steve! Help!”

Steve rushed in. Alex’s arrows passed overhead. The frost dragon covered the wall in blue ice. Together, they held the second line.

## The Shadow End King

They entered the deepest hall of the black fortress.

Silence filled the room.

Thump.

Thump.

Thump.

A giant black statue cracked open. Red eyes appeared inside. The Shadow End King rose with six black wings behind it.

It swung its spear. A column of darkness crashed toward them.

Steve raised the sky shield. “Get ready!”

In the first phase, the king summoned shadow dragons. Pangpang’s axe knocked one out of the air.

“See? I’m a professional now!”

Alex shattered the first shadow crystal. Steve’s sky sword collided with the black spear, and the shockwave broke the pillars around them.

In the second phase, the six wings began spinning the entire sky. Pangpang asked if the wind was too strong and was immediately blown away.

“I take it back!”

The frost dragon flew into the center of the storm. Its wings were still young, but its blue light grew brighter. A breath of ice crossed the clouds and froze the storm into a bridge.

Steve charged across it. Alex fired the storm bow. Pangpang fell from the sky and landed beside the king’s feet.

The king looked down.

Pangpang looked up. “What are you staring at? Keep fighting.”

In the third phase, the sky split open. A final dark core appeared in the king’s chest. The amulet showed one sentence:

> The core is the weakness.

Steve raised the sky sword. Alex drew the storm bow. Pangpang recovered his axe. The frost dragon climbed to the highest cloud and dived.

Sword, bow, axe and blue-white dragon breath struck the core at once.

The first crack was quiet.

The second shook the fortress.

After the third, the core shattered into black sparks.

The Shadow End King fell to its knees.

“True strength has never been power…”

Its voice faded into the storm.

“It is trust between you.”

Then it dissolved like black snow over the clouds.

## Day One Hundred: Homeward

The sun rose behind the clouds. The darkness vanished. The sky turned blue again.

The amulet glowed gold:

> Hundred-day trial complete.

Steve, Alex, Pangpang and the frost dragon stood above the clouds.

“Where should we go next?” Pangpang asked.

“Home first,” said Alex.

Steve nodded. “Home is always where an adventure begins.”

The dragon landed on Steve’s shoulder and brushed his cheek.

Pangpang looked at its growing wings. “Wait. Is it bigger than me now?”

Steve laughed. “It will always be our partner.”

They climbed onto the dragon and flew over the clouds, the floating islands and the road home.

The hundred-day adventure was complete.

The next adventure had just begun.
