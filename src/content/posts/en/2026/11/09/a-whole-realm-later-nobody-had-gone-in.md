Steve had not even punched a tree when a stack of diamonds fell from the sky.

Sixty-four.

Alex watched the sky for a moment. Once she was sure a second stack was not immediately following, she crouched to help collect them.

This Minecraft save had custom rules for both equipment and adventure realms. The three took their diamonds to a village, bought a house, equipped themselves with diamond armor, spears, and shields, and started carrying in furniture.

Pangpang had just decided where the bed should go when another thump came from outside.

A stack of gold this time.

He set down the bed and ran to the door.

The third delivery was larger: half a stack each of enchanted golden apples, Nether stars, netherite ingots, and all-attribute enchanted books. Thirty-two of each. Other treasures lay in a heap too large to count at once.

The furniture was still unfinished. The three began changing their equipment instead.

_The bed still had no place. The equipment was already changing._

## One More Set of Armor

Beyond the village stood an enormous obsidian wall with a portal set into it.

A broadcast announced a new realm. It would remain open for a long time. There were good things inside.

Steve felt his armor.

Alex checked her weapon.

Pangpang, who had recently felt invincible, stood a little straighter.

“Are we still a bit short on equipment?”

Nobody disagreed.

They had already been preparing to fight the dragon. Now, at the realm's entrance, they discovered yet another portal beside it.

This one led to the Sieve Realm.

Sieve, as in the thing you sift with.

Inside, they could mine a custom material called Decline Alloy. Getting enough took a tremendous effort. They brought back a large supply and replaced their equipment all over again.

Full Decline Alloy sets this time.

They still had not entered the main realm.

Pangpang rolled his shoulders inside his new armor.

“This should do it.”

## Red Means Lowest Tier

A hundred-person team in alloy armor had already gathered outside the Chaos Realm.

The three compared equipment with them. Their own enchantments were only slightly better.

Their brand-new gear suddenly seemed less remarkable.

They advanced with the team. The first monsters to meet them were red.

The lowest tier.

One lunged. Its claws caught alloy armor and left a deep gouge straight across it.

Steve glanced behind him.

There was no lower tier for them to practice on first.

![A low-tier red monster gouges Steve's silvery alloy armor as Alex and Pangpang raise their shields in surprise](/uploads/2026/11/09/%E7%A7%98%E5%A2%83%E8%B5%B0%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E5%9C%88%EF%BC%8C%E8%B0%81%E4%B9%9F%E6%B2%A1%E8%BF%9B%E5%8E%BB/scene-2.webp)

_Even the lowest tier was enough to gouge their armor._

The team fought its way forward until, at last, they reached Lake Hylia.

The water was clear.

Fresh water, with a pleasant shore. Eyes that had been fixed on monster claws could finally rest on the lake.

Pangpang crouched, stood again, and relaxed his shoulders a little.

“Made it?”

Alex found a large map nearby.

“To the entrance.”

The map showed five regions. An underwater castle occupied the center, with the other areas spread around it. The lake was still in the very first entrance area.

After all that fighting, they had not reached the realm's final destination at all.

![On the clear shore of Lake Hylia, the three study a five-region map with an underwater castle in the center and the entrance at its edge](/uploads/2026/11/09/%E7%A7%98%E5%A2%83%E8%B5%B0%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E5%9C%88%EF%BC%8C%E8%B0%81%E4%B9%9F%E6%B2%A1%E8%BF%9B%E5%8E%BB/scene-3.webp)

_Lovely water, lovely shore. Still only the entrance._

## Nothing on the Map Looked Simple

The birdlike Rito lived to the left. The Gerudo lived in the desert, and the Zora by the water. Above them, around the volcano, lived the Gulong tribe.

The Gulong were sturdy stone people. They even ate stone.

All four peoples were friendly, provided players did not provoke them.

One teammate apparently needed to test that. He spat on a Gulong.

Several stone people immediately approached, bound him, and struck him down into the fading particles of a game defeat.

The rest of the team went quiet.

Pangpang lowered the hand he had just raised to point out the way.

Later, they encountered someone being treated as a king. He made quite an impression, then went down after only a few hammer blows.

A fake.

The real leader was someone else. On the volcano stood something else again: an enormous mechanical fire salamander called Darunia.

From a distance, smoke rose from the mountain, with its mechanical outline crouched against the slope. It looked nothing like the fellow who had lasted only a few blows.

![The three stand on a rocky overlook before a volcano, looking up at the huge mechanical fire salamander Darunia on its slope](/uploads/2026/11/09/%E7%A7%98%E5%A2%83%E8%B5%B0%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E5%9C%88%EF%BC%8C%E8%B0%81%E4%B9%9F%E6%B2%A1%E8%BF%9B%E5%8E%BB/scene-4.webp)

_That outline did not suggest a fight of just a few hammer blows._

The other regions had mechanical beasts too, but they were not all salamanders.

The thunder region had a camel. The water region had an elephant called Ruta. Before the three could establish what was in the wind region, another problem arrived.

Four syllables were trapped.

A leader asked them to find the syllables. The situation was urgent.

“Syllables?” Pangpang asked.

“Syllables,” Alex confirmed.

They had not finished studying the map. The road was already difficult. Now there was a task whose very name needed checking.

Steve gripped his weapon and waited for the next thing to happen.

Nothing happened.

## Look Down First

The lake, the map, the mechanical outline on the volcano: suddenly, none of it was right.

The three looked down.

They had been standing in the same place the entire time.

Everything that had just happened was imagined.

They had not fought their way to the lake. They had not hurried around five regions. The adventure had grown larger and larger without a single actual step.

They had not even entered the realm.

![Steve, Alex, and Pangpang remain outside the obsidian portal looking down at their feet as imagined lake and volcano shapes fade overhead](/uploads/2026/11/09/%E7%A7%98%E5%A2%83%E8%B5%B0%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E5%9C%88%EF%BC%8C%E8%B0%81%E4%B9%9F%E6%B2%A1%E8%BF%9B%E5%8E%BB/scene-5.webp)

_They had imagined the whole journey without crossing the threshold._

Steve looked at Alex. Alex looked at Pangpang.

Pangpang looked at his feet too.

“So, back when we said our equipment wasn't enough…”

Nobody answered.

The portal was still in front of them.

They had imagined every difficulty on the other side. Their feet had not moved at all.
