After equipping the village, the three finally went to the End.

Steve thought they might not win. He kept repeating the things they needed to watch for. Alex listened and did not hurry forward either.

Pangpang held his trident and looked up at the dragon.

In this custom world, events rarely followed the order they had prepared.

![The three enter the End with a trident, expecting a difficult battle](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%89%E6%88%9F%E5%9B%9E%E6%9D%91%EF%BC%8C%E5%90%8D%E5%AD%97%E5%B7%B2%E7%BB%8F%E5%8F%98%E4%BA%86/scene-1.webp)

_The dragon turns before the plan is finished._

As the dragon turned, Pangpang's trident flew out.

All three followed it with their eyes.

Straight into the dragon's head.

The Ender Dragon was defeated outright.

Steve had not finished putting away his worries, but the thing he had been worried about was already gone. Beside him, Alex was still holding her equipment in its ready-for-battle position.

Pangpang looked at his empty hand.

“That's it?”

![The flying trident strikes the dragon's head as it dissolves into light](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%89%E6%88%9F%E5%9B%9E%E6%9D%91%EF%BC%8C%E5%90%8D%E5%AD%97%E5%B7%B2%E7%BB%8F%E5%8F%98%E4%BA%86/scene-2.webp)

_The long battle never gets started._

Not quite.

The trident began to change. Its three points took a new shape. New blades extended beside the long shaft's tip. It became Lü Bu's legendary Fangtian Huaji, a crescent-bladed halberd.

Pangpang held it without quite understanding what had happened.

Steve leaned closer to the place where the old prongs had been. “So some ordinary weapons can evolve when certain things happen to them?”

Pangpang turned the weapon over. It certainly was not the same one anymore.

They had come to find out whether they could beat the dragon. Now even the weapon they took home needed a new introduction.

Back in the village, people noticed the familiar trio first, then the thing Pangpang was carrying. Some stared at the blade. Some walked around to the other side, then back again.

“Is that what you took with you?”

Pangpang shook his head. “It was a trident when we left.”

![Pangpang raises the transformed halberd as the village cheers](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%89%E6%88%9F%E5%9B%9E%E6%9D%91%EF%BC%8C%E5%90%8D%E5%AD%97%E5%B7%B2%E7%BB%8F%E5%8F%98%E4%BA%86/scene-3.webp)

_It left as a trident. It needs a new introduction._

Steve explained what had happened in the End. The longer he spoke, the harder the villagers found it to believe. When Pangpang raised the halberd, somebody in the front finally started clapping.

Then the next row. Then the people behind them.

Cheers filled the square and drowned out the questions. Pangpang opened his mouth to explain something else, started laughing, and simply kept the weapon raised.

Alex stood beside Steve and waited for a slight lull.

“Are you still going to explain that long battle plan?”

Steve looked at the weapon with its new name.

“No. If I tell it now, it'll sound like I made it up afterward.”
