In a new save, the three went mining the ordinary way.

Steve and Alex were just getting ready to work when a creeper fell from above and blasted them both back to spawn.

Pangpang was mining iron in another passage, safely outside the blast. He paused at the noise, looked at the iron in his hands, and finished the job.

By the time he left the cave, he had enough material for iron armor, an iron sword, and an iron pickaxe.

The others were still coming back. He was already dressed.

![Pangpang mines iron in another tunnel, away from the falling creeper](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%EF%BC%8C%E8%8B%B9%E6%9E%9C%E8%BF%98%E6%98%AF%E6%99%AE%E9%80%9A%E7%9A%84/scene-1.webp)

_A different tunnel. Finish mining first._

Pangpang went deeper and found three stacks of diamonds.

One hundred and ninety-two.

At the workbench, he listed the things he wanted: diamond armor, sword, pickaxe, axe, and shovel. This custom world also allowed a diamond spear, so he made that too. He added a shield separately.

Once the equipment covered the bench, he tried making a diamond apple.

Steve stared at its blue gleam for a while.

“Are you going mining, or taking the mine out for a walk?”

Pangpang checked his equipment carefully. Everything was there.

![Pangpang checks his diamond armor, tools and custom spear](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%EF%BC%8C%E8%8B%B9%E6%9E%9C%E8%BF%98%E6%98%AF%E6%99%AE%E9%80%9A%E7%9A%84/scene-2.webp)

_Three stacks of diamonds. Lay out the equipment first._

While stocking up in the village, he added one stack of golden apples and one stack of bread. Sixty-four of each. His pack felt reassuringly prepared.

Alex looked at the iron armor he had taken off.

Pangpang handed the old set straight to her. He was wearing diamond now; somebody else could use the iron.

Steve stayed where he was, watching the exchange. Then his eyes returned to the diamond apple.

Its blue shine was fading.

The diamond apple became an ordinary apple.

![Pangpang gives Alex his old iron armor as the diamond apple turns ordinary](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%EF%BC%8C%E8%8B%B9%E6%9E%9C%E8%BF%98%E6%98%AF%E6%99%AE%E9%80%9A%E7%9A%84/scene-3.webp)

_The iron armor changes hands. The apple returns to normal._

Pangpang turned it over and back again. Red. Ordinary. Just like any other apple.

He checked his diamond armor again, then the sword and pickaxe.

Fortunately, those had stayed put.

Alex held her iron armor securely. Steve looked from her to Pangpang, then pointed at the apple.

“No need to check that item again.”
