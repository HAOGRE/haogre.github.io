The three stopped as soon as they reached the Nether fortress entrance.

An army of thirty stood ahead.

Netherite from helmets to boots, every set decorated with gold trim. Their formation left the entrance shimmering with gold-edged lines.

Steve looked at the army, then at his two friends.

Their armor was good. So was the other side's.

Pangpang pulled his foot back.

![The three adventurers spot a gold-trimmed netherite army at the fortress](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%AA%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%A8%E6%9D%91%E6%8D%A2%E7%94%B2/scene-1.webp)

_Thirty gold-trimmed sets. Do not rush in._

They did not charge. They hurried home to warn the village and prepare first.

When it was time to set out again, three iron golems went into the Nether.

Three.

Steve counted once more from behind. Thirty on that side. Three on this one.

The numbers looked alarming. The fight did not follow them. The golems entered the Nether, met the gold-trimmed formation, and broke the entire thirty-strong army. None remained.

![Three iron golems enter the Nether to face the army](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%AA%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%A8%E6%9D%91%E6%8D%A2%E7%94%B2/scene-2.webp)

_Only three golems go out. The preparations came first._

Afterward, everyone brought their available equipment and materials together for a proper inventory.

Pangpang stopped halfway through counting.

Alex checked again. Steve went to check the villagers, then returned to compare the totals.

There was enough to equip every villager with a full netherite set.

“Everyone?” Steve asked.

“Everyone.”

They did not stay at the ledger celebrating. They started work.

For three days and three nights, somebody was always at the smithing table. Armor plates moved aside, helmets lined up, leggings and boots matched into sets. At first Steve looked up to check the time of day. Later, he only checked what the next set was missing.

Only after the third night did they finally manage to finish them all.

![After three days and nights, the three friends distribute netherite armor](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%AA%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%A8%E6%9D%91%E6%8D%A2%E7%94%B2/scene-3.webp)

_This time, the list makes sure nobody is left out._

At the distribution, the villagers first reached out quietly. Then they saw their neighbors receiving full sets too, and laughter spread through the square.

Some tried their helmets first. Some held their chestplates without putting them down. The three worked down the list until, at last, nobody had to hear, “Wait for the next batch.”

The whole village was delighted.

Steve looked across the square at all the new armor and lifted a hand to pat his own chestplate. Halfway there, he lowered it again.

His arm ached.

Pangpang leaned against the table, equally unwilling to move. Alex checked the last empty place on the list and closed it.

For once, the three of them were the ones who needed a rest.
