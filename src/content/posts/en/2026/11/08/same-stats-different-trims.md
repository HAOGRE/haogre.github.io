Pangpang found nothing on his first trip to the Nether.

No netherite. No Nether quartz. At least his diamond armor came home in one piece.

The next day, he tried the fortress again and finally found one piece of ancient debris.

He tucked it into his pack without telling Steve or Alex. Assuming he had come back empty-handed again, they each put one gold ingot in their own packs and hurried away.

Pangpang stayed behind, touched the hidden block through his pack, and said nothing for the moment.

![Pangpang hides one piece of ancient debris in his pack](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E5%B1%9E%E6%80%A7%E4%B8%80%E6%A0%B7%EF%BC%8C%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%E5%90%84%E9%80%89%E5%90%84%E7%9A%84/scene-1.webp)

_Nothing yesterday. One piece today. For now, say nothing._

When they gathered at the smithing table again, the find finally came out.

Choosing the upgrade order was less straightforward.

Steve examined the iron sword Alex offered, compared it with the table, and set it back down.

“An iron sword can't upgrade directly to netherite.”

Alex put it away. Pangpang's diamond armor was a suitable base, so they changed the plan: process the materials first, then start the armor upgrades with him.

![The iron sword stops at the smithing table as they discuss upgrading diamond armor](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E5%B1%9E%E6%80%A7%E4%B8%80%E6%A0%B7%EF%BC%8C%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%E5%90%84%E9%80%89%E5%90%84%E7%9A%84/scene-2.webp)

_Check the equipment base before choosing the upgrade order._

That one piece of debris was only a beginning. It could not magically supply a whole set. Later, Pangpang separately found several netherite ingots, making the further upgrades possible.

Helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots. Piece by piece. Eventually Steve and Alex had new armor too.

The three sat down to check the enchantments: Protection IV, Unbreaking III, Mending I.

The same stats.

Steve checked once, then looked at the lines on Pangpang's armor. “Why is yours purple, then?”

Pangpang touched his chestplate. Amethyst trim.

Steve looked down at his own diamond trim. Redstone-colored lines ran along Alex's armor. This custom save even offered redstone trim.

![Three equal netherite sets carry amethyst, diamond and redstone trims](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E5%B1%9E%E6%80%A7%E4%B8%80%E6%A0%B7%EF%BC%8C%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%E5%90%84%E9%80%89%E5%90%84%E7%9A%84/scene-3.webp)

_The stats match. The colors do not._

Alex had them set their helmets together.

Purple, blue, red. Easy to tell apart.

Steve read the enchantments again, confirming that the extra purple lines had not given Pangpang an extra stat.

Pangpang took his helmet back. “They're all the same.”

“I know.” Steve put on the blue-trimmed one. “Just making sure I don't take the wrong helmet.”
