The mutton and diamonds were safely stored beneath the cottage. The next day, Steve remembered the map and notebook that had arrived with the supplies.

He unfolded the map, turned it over, then turned it back.

Blank paper.

He tucked it into a corner. Alex found coordinates in the notebook instead. The three studied them, then looked straight down.

Right beneath their feet.

They dug with excited cries, not even stopping to cover the opening above. There really was a small chest below.

Inside: one stack of sticks. Sixty-four, neatly bundled.

Steve crouched there as though something else might spring out of the bottom. Nothing did.

![A chest beneath their feet contains only one stack of sticks](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E8%A1%A5%E7%BB%99%E6%97%A0%E9%99%90%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%B2%E8%92%82%E5%A4%AB%E7%85%A7%E5%B8%B8%E9%87%8D%E7%94%9F/scene-1.webp)

_The coordinates were exact. The expectations were not._

His complaints reached the sky.

Whoever kept dropping things apparently objected and sent another notebook. Steve and Alex opened it as usual. A miniature creeper emerged.

“You can fit that in—”

Pop.

The blast was small, but it left both of them dizzy. Pangpang was sleeping off to the side. He had not leaned over the book and missed the fright entirely.

![A miniature creeper startles Steve and Alex while Pangpang sleeps nearby](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E8%A1%A5%E7%BB%99%E6%97%A0%E9%99%90%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%B2%E8%92%82%E5%A4%AB%E7%85%A7%E5%B8%B8%E9%87%8D%E7%94%9F/scene-2.webp)

_A small blast. Not enough to wake everyone._

Steve shouted angrily at the sky. Something powerful came down and sent him back to respawn in the cottage.

When the next delivery appeared, he dodged first.

This time it was two golden apples.

Alex took one. Pangpang took one. By the time Steve came out of hiding, all he could do was watch them with one each.

He started shouting again.

An enormous hammer came down. Steve respawned once more. The hammer disappeared afterward, leaving him nothing.

Two apples for the others. One respawn for him.

![Alex and Pangpang each get a golden apple while a hammer looms over Steve](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E8%A1%A5%E7%BB%99%E6%97%A0%E9%99%90%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%B2%E8%92%82%E5%A4%AB%E7%85%A7%E5%B8%B8%E9%87%8D%E7%94%9F/scene-3.webp)

_Both apples are taken. The third delivery comes from above._

At last they decided to go mining as usual.

Alex and Pangpang kept finding iron and coal. After a while, neither was impressed. “Still more of this?”

They went to see whether Steve was having better luck. He had barely entered his tunnel before a creeper blasted him back to spawn.

The two stood beside their iron and coal. Suddenly, the haul looked quite respectable.

The lucky ones were over here.

![Alex and Pangpang look from their iron and coal toward smoke in Steve's tunnel](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E8%A1%A5%E7%BB%99%E6%97%A0%E9%99%90%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%B2%E8%92%82%E5%A4%AB%E7%85%A7%E5%B8%B8%E9%87%8D%E7%94%9F/scene-4.webp)

_No diamonds does not necessarily mean bad luck._

Back home, mutton went on the table again.

Steve was growing tired of it. Before he could finish saying so, a gigantic chest landed on him. He went to respawn; Alex and Pangpang went to open it.

Three stacks of bread, with ten stacks of chicken behind them. They carried everything into the cellar. By the time Steve returned, the delivery had been put away.

Then they noticed something stranger. No matter how much mutton they ate, thirty stacks remained. The bread stayed at three stacks; the chicken stayed at ten.

In this prank-loving save, the food supply simply did not shrink.

Before Steve could properly enjoy the news, another enormous chest sent him back to spawn. Thirty stacks of beef this time. Alex and Pangpang stored it behind the mutton. They had become very efficient.

![Food fills the cellar while Steve holds a note delivered from the sky](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E8%A1%A5%E7%BB%99%E6%97%A0%E9%99%90%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%B2%E8%92%82%E5%A4%AB%E7%85%A7%E5%B8%B8%E9%87%8D%E7%94%9F/scene-5.webp)

_The stock stays full. The note shows no mercy._

A note floated down at last.

Lightweight this time. No chest. No hammer. Alex caught it and read it aloud.

“You keep yelling at me. Who else am I supposed to drop things on?”

Steve looked at the note, then at the cellar full of food.

The supplies really were unlimited.

He just had to walk out of the cottage all over again whenever they arrived.
