This new save delivered its welcome package from the sky.

Steve, Alex, and Pangpang had barely spawned when a wooden chest thumped onto the grass. It was full of chicken drumsticks.

Pangpang counted the first stack: sixty-four. Then he counted the stacks: twenty.

“Twenty times sixty-four,” Alex said. “One thousand two hundred and eighty.”

Steve shut the lid and opened it again. The number stayed the same.

![Three new adventurers gather around a sky-delivered chest of chicken](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E7%A0%B4%E6%9C%A8%E5%B1%8B%E4%B8%8B%E9%9D%A2%EF%BC%8C%E6%89%8D%E6%98%AF%E4%BB%93%E5%BA%93/scene-1.webp)

_Twenty stacks of sixty-four. The surprise begins with arithmetic._

They carried their absurd beginner supplies to a nearby village. The villagers checked the arithmetic too, looked at the chest, then at the three poorly equipped adventurers, and gave each of them a full set of diamond armor.

Armor acquired, small cottage purchased. They were ready to leave when another enormous chest fell before they had taken a single step.

Mutton this time.

Thirty stacks. One thousand nine hundred and twenty pieces.

Pangpang stared at the chest, then at the doorway. Getting rich had not made the door any wider.

“We'll keep it at home and eat it slowly,” Steve said.

Inside, Alex turned and bumped into something. “First tell me where the people are supposed to stand.”

![The three adventurers in diamond armor face a huge chest of mutton outside their small cottage](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E7%A0%B4%E6%9C%A8%E5%B1%8B%E4%B8%8B%E9%9D%A2%EF%BC%8C%E6%89%8D%E6%98%AF%E4%BB%93%E5%BA%93/scene-2.webp)

_Thirty stacks of mutton arrive. The cottage stays the same size._

Steve dealt with the chicken first. He sold all of it for some iron ingots and basic tools.

The villagers in this world were happy to make that trade. Diamond armor was useful, but it did not replace a proper set of everyday tools.

With the chicken gone, they could finally walk across the room. Thirty stacks of mutton still needed a home, though, and their pile of diamonds could not stay spread across the floor.

Pangpang looked down.

The house was small. The space underneath was not.

They dug out a roomy cellar, carried the mutton chests below, and tucked the diamonds farther down. Steve passed things down the ladder, Alex took them, and Pangpang shifted the chests into place. The fuller the cellar became, the poorer the cottage looked.

![A spacious cellar of mutton and diamonds lies beneath a shabby cottage](/uploads/2026/11/08/%E7%A0%B4%E6%9C%A8%E5%B1%8B%E4%B8%8B%E9%9D%A2%EF%BC%8C%E6%89%8D%E6%98%AF%E4%BB%93%E5%BA%93/scene-3.webp)

_The house is upstairs. The fortune is downstairs._

At last they climbed upstairs and secured the entrance.

From outside, it was still an ordinary shabby wooden cottage. Old boards. Small rooms. Nothing worth a second glance.

Steve stood in the doorway, satisfied.

Pangpang peered inside. “If someone comes to view the house, do they have to ask whether the downstairs is open?”

Alex shut the door.

“It isn't. That's where the good stuff is.”
