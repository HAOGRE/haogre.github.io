_The diamonds stay. The cake is compensation._

While they counted supplies, Steve found himself watching the diamonds again.

Thirty-two for Alex. Thirty-two for Pangpang.

He studied them for a moment and thought perhaps his turn was next. As soon as he moved his hand, both pulled their own diamonds against their chests.

Thirty-two each. None available.

Steve's hand stopped halfway.

“I thought there was compensation?”

Alex freed one hand and pushed over a portion of cake. Pangpang pushed over another.

Two portions arrived in front of Steve.

“This is it.”

He looked down at the cake, then at the diamonds they were still guarding so closely. He had been waiting for something blue to move his way. What arrived was edible.

He redirected his hand.

At least this time, something in front of him was actually his.

Later, when the three went mining, he discovered that those thirty-two diamonds had not been the whole story either.

He was still wearing his old equipment. Alex and Pangpang already had diamond armor, conspicuously bright as they walked.

Steve looked at their armor and remembered the diamonds they had protected during the count.

“Didn't you each have thirty-two?”

“Those are extra.”

Steve stopped.

They had already set aside a substantial supply of diamonds to make equipment for the two of them. The piles they had counted earlier were simply additional stock.

He had thought he was looking at all their belongings. He had only seen the spending money.

Steve looked down at his old gear, then up at them.

The two portions of cake were real.

The thirty-two diamonds were real too.

His calculation had simply left out two entire suits of armor.
