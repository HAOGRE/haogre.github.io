The three characters had barely spawned in a new save when Pangpang began assigning jobs.

“Steve gets wood, Alex gets stone, and I—”

A zombie came around the corner.

Before Pangpang could give himself a job, all three were looking at the respawn screen.

“What were you in charge of?” Alex asked.

“Starting over, apparently.”

![A zombie interrupts the trio’s first plans.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E9%93%81%E5%A4%B4%EF%BC%8C%E6%8D%A2%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E5%87%BA%E7%94%9F%E7%82%B9/scene-1.webp)

_The opening ended before the job assignments did._

In this custom save, respawning put them beside a village. They opened their eyes to fields, houses, and a welcome absence of that zombie.

Steve checked his inventory. Empty.

Pangpang checked his twice, as though another look might make something appear.

“Stop checking,” Alex said. “Find a crafting table.”

This time they skipped the meeting about who should do what. Steve collected wood. Alex gathered useful materials. Pangpang finally received his assignment: watch the furnace while the iron smelted.

He brought over a stone block to sit on and watched closely.

“What does staring at it accomplish?”

“At least it won’t sneak up behind me.”

![The trio gathers materials while Pangpang watches the furnace.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E9%93%81%E5%A4%B4%EF%BC%8C%E6%8D%A2%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E5%87%BA%E7%94%9F%E7%82%B9/scene-2.webp)

_This time, work came before discussion._

They soon had the iron they needed.

Steve put on the first helmet and stepped away from the crafting table. Alex made the second. Pangpang waited for the last.

When the final iron-gray block settled onto Pangpang’s head, he tapped its rim.

“Full iron armor. Done.”

Alex looked down at his shirt.

“Iron helmets.”

Pangpang inspected his legs and shoes and revised his announcement. “Heads first.”

![The trio wears matching iron helmets over ordinary clothes at the village gate.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E9%93%81%E5%A4%B4%EF%BC%8C%E6%8D%A2%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E5%87%BA%E7%94%9F%E7%82%B9/scene-3.webp)

_Three helmets matched; three full suits of armor did not._

At the village gate, the three heads finally matched.

Steve reached for his sword, ready to look outside. Pangpang moved a step behind him.

“What if that zombie’s still there?”

“We have helmets this time.”

“It didn’t ask last time.”

The gate fell quiet for a moment.

Alex pushed it open, then stopped. “Food first.”

Pangpang turned back at once. This time his job was perfectly clear.
