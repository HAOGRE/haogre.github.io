After the village was almost flattened, the three moved into the cave where they had taken shelter.

This time, it held more than stone.

Alex carried a torch deeper inside and spotted a fleck of blue in the wall. She moved the light. There was more.

Steve picked up his iron pickaxe. Pangpang cleared space previously occupied by rubble. They followed the vein until, gathered together, their find amounted to more than three hundred diamonds.

Pangpang looked from the diamonds to the ruins outside.

“We can build the village again.”

![The trio discovers more than three hundred diamonds in the sheltering cave.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E5%9F%8B%E5%9C%A8%E5%B1%8B%E4%B8%8B%EF%BC%8C%E5%A2%99%E7%94%A8%E5%B9%B3%E6%BB%91%E7%9F%B3%E5%A4%B4/scene-1.webp)

_The cave first sheltered them, then yielded diamonds._

At first, they meant to put diamonds on the walls.

Alex drew a gate and houses in the dirt. Pangpang circled the best-looking spots. Steve kept adding walls that still needed drawing.

The more he added, the smaller the supply looked.

“Didn’t we say more than three hundred?” Pangpang asked.

“Diamonds,” Alex said. “You still need enough to make blocks. And there’s more than this one stretch of wall.”

Steve put down the diamonds he had been about to fit into place.

They studied the plan, then began dismantling.

Every diamond already used came back down and joined the rest. Pangpang checked the corner one last time. Even the little patch of blue he had most wanted to keep was removed.

![All the removed diamonds are stored deep beneath their house.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E5%9F%8B%E5%9C%A8%E5%B1%8B%E4%B8%8B%EF%BC%8C%E5%A2%99%E7%94%A8%E5%B9%B3%E6%BB%91%E7%9F%B3%E5%A4%B4/scene-2.webp)

_Every patch of blue came off the walls._

They dug deep beneath their own house, stored the diamonds there, and sealed the space above.

Back on the surface, Pangpang looked down, moved a couple of steps, and looked again.

“You can’t tell.”

Alex handed him a stone block. “Then let’s build the part you can see.”

Ordinary stone replaced the diamonds. They smelted and worked it, then laid rows of smooth stone. It lacked that conspicuous blue, but the joints were neat. Windows and doorframes gradually took shape.

Steve carried materials. Alex checked the lines. Pangpang inspected what they had just built.

This time, he did not ask to keep a diamond in the wall.

“This edge still needs smoothing,” he said instead.

![Villagers move into the smooth-stone village as the trio checks a block beneath a window.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E5%9F%8B%E5%9C%A8%E5%B1%8B%E4%B8%8B%EF%BC%8C%E5%A2%99%E7%94%A8%E5%B9%B3%E6%BB%91%E7%9F%B3%E5%A4%B4/scene-3.webp)

_Ordinary stone made handsome walls, too._

Once the new village could house people, they invited villagers to move in.

Footsteps passed through the gate and along the level road. Some newcomers stopped to examine the walls; others went to find their houses. Belongings began appearing beside doors that had stood empty.

Pangpang stood outside their own house and looked up.

No diamonds on the wall. None beside the door, either.

“Want to put them back?” Alex asked.

Pangpang ran his hand over the smooth stone.

“No. This looks good.”

Steve was about to put down his tools when Pangpang pointed below a window.

“Except that block. Is it lined up properly?”

The diamonds lay quietly underground. Above them, three people gathered around an ordinary piece of stone to discuss it again.
