The three iron helmets had barely been fitted when green shapes began falling from the sky.

Pangpang looked up, then immediately ducked.

“Those aren’t leaves.”

Steve was already pulling him away. A creeper landed where they had stood. Its hiss stopped, and dirt sailed over their heads.

This custom save dropped mobs from the sky. Evidently, the zombie at spawn had not been the complete introduction.

![Three stacks of creepers fall toward the village.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E8%BF%98%E6%B2%A1%E6%95%B0%E5%AE%8C%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%88%E6%9D%A5%E4%B8%89%E7%99%BE%E5%8F%AA/scene-1.webp)

_Three stacks, not three creepers._

“Three stacks!” Alex shouted.

Pangpang tried to calculate while running. “Three creepers?”

“Three stacks.”

At sixty-four per stack, that made one hundred and ninety-two. Steve did not stop to verify the count. Green shapes filled the gaps between houses. Avoiding them came first.

They tried repairing a fence. No sooner had a block been placed than another hole opened nearby. Moving from one wall to the next, they discovered the real challenge: deciding which wall would still exist a second later.

Pangpang touched his helmet. “We have iron heads. We still need a roof.”

The light above him dimmed again.

![Another wave appears before the trio can finish repairs.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E8%BF%98%E6%B2%A1%E6%95%B0%E5%AE%8C%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%88%E6%9D%A5%E4%B8%89%E7%99%BE%E5%8F%AA/scene-2.webp)

_The sky darkens before the fence is repaired._

The next wave was three hundred.

“Stop counting!” Steve shouted. “The cave!”

Explosions ran together along the village edge. The three hurried beside broken walls. What had been a recognizable road was now scattered stone and craters.

Alex looked back. Half a roof was gone.

Pangpang looked back. There was no longer much point checking the other half.

He stopped discussing whether the helmets were enough and followed Steve toward the cave.

![The trio reaches the cave as explosions continue outside.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E8%BF%98%E6%B2%A1%E6%95%B0%E5%AE%8C%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%88%E6%9D%A5%E4%B8%89%E7%99%BE%E5%8F%AA/scene-3.webp)

_This time, getting inside came first._

By the time they reached it, their health was low enough that nobody wanted to test another hit.

Steve shifted a stone out of the way. Alex slipped through. Pangpang squeezed in last. Another blast sounded behind him, shaking dust from the ceiling.

They retreated farther inside and waited against the rock.

Pangpang felt for his helmet, then checked again.

“Still here.”

“So are we,” Alex said.

Steve watched the entrance. “Let’s wait before checking on anything else.”

The noise outside continued. Nobody suggested returning for the chests.

![After the smoke clears, the trio faces an almost flattened village.](/uploads/2026/11/17/%E4%B8%89%E7%BB%84%E8%BF%98%E6%B2%A1%E6%95%B0%E5%AE%8C%EF%BC%8C%E5%8F%88%E6%9D%A5%E4%B8%89%E7%99%BE%E5%8F%AA/scene-4.webp)

_Almost no village remained, but all three were still there._

When the smoke cleared, they stepped out.

The village was almost flattened. A few wall foundations still marked the houses, but fields and paths had become difficult to distinguish among the craters.

After a long silence, Pangpang pointed at a broken wall. “Was our house over there?”

Steve studied it. “They all look rather similar now.”

They turned toward the cave.

Its entrance remained. They had not returned to spawn.

Alex set their tools on an unbroken stone.

“Find somewhere level first.”

Pangpang checked the sky for another patch of green. Then he crouched and picked up the first piece of broken wood by his feet.
