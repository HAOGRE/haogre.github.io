Pangpang said he would take whatever fell from the sky.

Ali heard him up above.

A dark object promptly dropped to the ground. Pangpang dodged aside and watched it for a while.

No explosion.

On closer inspection, it was a model bomb.

Steve came over. Alex came over too. All three stood around the model without rushing to touch it first.

A note drifted down next.

“Didn't you say you'd take anything? I'll drop a real bomb for you next.”

Pangpang finished reading and looked up.

He withdrew the relief he had felt a moment earlier.

![Pangpang crouches beside a wooden model bomb, looking up from a drifting note while Steve and Alex watch](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%8B%E4%B8%80%E9%A2%97%E4%B9%9F%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84/scene-1.webp)

_The fake was identified. The real one was announced._

## The Next One Really Was Real

The following day, Ali did return.

This time he dropped one real bomb.

When the noise stopped, Pangpang was still there.

Covered in dust and soot.

Steve looked at his hair. Alex looked at his clothes. Pangpang brushed himself twice, raising enough dust to make both friends take half a step back.

“No need to test this one,” he said.

A model would not leave him looking like that.

![After one real bomb, Pangpang brushes dust from his clothes as Steve and Alex avoid the rising cloud](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%8B%E4%B8%80%E9%A2%97%E4%B9%9F%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84/scene-2.webp)

_No further authenticity test was needed._

## Three This Time

Later, Ali passed overhead again.

By the time Pangpang looked up, three bombs were already coming down.

Another burst of noise.

Another coating of dust.

Pangpang wiped his face. It removed very little, mainly moving the dust somewhere else. He stood still, waiting for Ali to say something.

Ali flew away.

![Dust-covered Pangpang watches Ali fly away beside three shallow blast pits; all three have empty hands and the wooden model remains intact](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%8B%E4%B8%80%E9%A2%97%E4%B9%9F%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84/scene-3.webp)

_Three bombs later, he flew away. No flatbread followed._

Not a word.

Certainly no flatbread.

Pangpang watched the receding figure. His mouth opened, then closed.

Steve and Alex stood beside him. Neither found anything useful to say.

The model was still perfectly intact.

Pangpang looked at it, then brushed his clothes again.

He could now tell the real thing from the fake quite clearly.
