Steve heard something overhead. He looked up, then stepped aside.

Ten bombs fell. A house disappeared.

Nobody was hurt.

The three walked around the wreckage. Pangpang picked up a usable piece of timber and sighed.

“Better carrying this than carrying people.”

They cleared the rubble, salvaged materials, rebuilt the walls, and replaced the roof. At last, the building looked like a house again.

Then ten more bombs fell from the sky.

This time the blacksmith's shop disappeared.

Again, nobody was hurt.

Steve looked at his tools, then at the fresh rubble.

No need to put the tools away.

_The house was rebuilt. The tools still could not be put away._

## Rebuild the Furnace First

The smithy needed rebuilding too.

They cleared a space and set up the furnace and work area again. Pangpang had just straightened his back beside the new shop when another sound came from above.

He immediately stepped back.

This delivery was blue.

A stack of diamonds. Sixty-four.

Steve started forward to collect them. The entire stack landed in the new smithy's furnace instead.

All three faces fell a little.

Before they could sigh, blue light appeared below the furnace.

In this custom Minecraft save, the furnace could melt and cast diamonds. Bright blue liquid flowed along channels into the sword molds beside it.

One blade after another.

Thirty in all.

![The rebuilt smithy's furnace receives falling diamonds and pours glowing blue liquid into sword molds as the three watch in surprise](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E9%93%81%E5%8C%A0%E9%93%BA%E5%88%9A%E5%BB%BA%E5%A5%BD%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%89%E5%8D%81%E6%8A%8A%E5%89%91%E5%87%BA%E7%82%89/scene-2.webp)

_They missed catching the diamonds. The sword molds caught the result._

## Sharp Without an Enchantment

Steve leaned closer.

No enchantment glint.

But these were exceptionally well-made diamond swords. A careful test showed an edge comparable to a diamond sword enchanted with Sharpness.

Alex laid one flat and aligned the next beside it. Blue blades gradually filled the workbench in the new smithy.

Pangpang's intended sigh became a sharp intake of breath.

“Thirty?”

Steve counted again.

“Thirty.”

![A batch of unenchanted diamond swords fills the new smithy's bench as the three finish handles and arrange the blades](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E9%93%81%E5%8C%A0%E9%93%BA%E5%88%9A%E5%BB%BA%E5%A5%BD%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%89%E5%8D%81%E6%8A%8A%E5%89%91%E5%87%BA%E7%82%89/scene-3.webp)

_Thirty swords. At last, work for the new smithy._

The three got straight to work, helping finish and store the formed blades. A moment ago, they had feared losing the diamonds in the furnace. Now they needed room for the swords coming out of it.

Tools in hand, Pangpang looked back at the sky.

“This delivery can stay.”

Alex moved the swords farther inside.

The new smithy was finally doing its job.

Preferably without being rebuilt again first.
