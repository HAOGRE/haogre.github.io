This time, three hundred bombs fell from the sky.

By the time Steve looked up, Pangpang was already taking cover. Alex watched the falling shapes, waiting for the noise to reach the village.

It did not.

Every bomb landed outside.

Explosions followed one another. Dirt flew upward and smoke rolled outward. The village stayed exactly where it was, untouched even at its edge.

Pangpang peered around his cover, looking from the entrance to the ground beyond it.

“Three hundred.”

Alex nodded.

“Not one came in.”

_Three hundred landed outside. The village edge stayed intact._

## Something New Outside

When the noise stopped, Steve went to inspect the damage.

There were diamonds in the blast crater.

Three hundred.

He crouched, looked behind another piece of stone, then turned back to call the village.

The people who had just been looking for cover began looking for pickaxes instead. Some brought tools. Others brought empty chests. The entrance grew busy at once.

In this Minecraft save with its unusually large ore veins, that patch of blue was plainly more than one pickaxe could collect quickly.

![Steve finds diamond ore in the crater outside the village as Alex, Pangpang, and villagers arrive with pickaxes and empty chests](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E4%B8%89%E7%99%BE%E9%A2%97%E7%82%B8%E5%BC%B9%EF%BC%8C%E8%BF%9E%E6%9D%91%E8%BE%B9%E9%83%BD%E6%B2%A1%E7%A2%B0%E5%88%B0/scene-2.webp)

_First they looked for cover. Now they looked for pickaxes._

With the whole village at work, they gradually collected the first three hundred diamonds.

The vein did not end.

Once the outer layer was mined, more remained inside. A layer of stone came away, exposing more blue. Chests carried out grew fuller; the supply of empty chests going in grew smaller.

Pangpang poked his head out of the mine.

“Still digging?”

Steve pointed behind him.

There were more in the wall.

## Three Hundred Was No Longer Enough

A later count put the total at over a thousand diamonds.

Three hundred was the number first discovered, not the entire deposit.

Alex checked the stored diamonds again. Pangpang sat beside an empty chest, looking from his pickaxe to the village.

![Alex counts diamonds in full chests while dusty Steve and Pangpang pause beside them; the village remains intact](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E4%B8%89%E7%99%BE%E9%A2%97%E7%82%B8%E5%BC%B9%EF%BC%8C%E8%BF%9E%E6%9D%91%E8%BE%B9%E9%83%BD%E6%B2%A1%E7%A2%B0%E5%88%B0/scene-3.webp)

_Three hundred at first sight. Over a thousand collected._

The village was still intact.

There were more diamonds on the ground and more mining dust on their hands. People who had run to hide from bombs were now running to carry chests.

Pangpang glanced at the sky, then moved the chest farther inside.

“Put these away first.”

Three hundred bombs had missed even the edge of the village.

They had still managed to bring the whole village outside.
