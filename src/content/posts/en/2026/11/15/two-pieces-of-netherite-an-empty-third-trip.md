A new save had barely begun. Before Steve, Alex, and Pangpang could punch a tree, a falling chest sent all three back to spawn.

They returned and opened it. Cooked mutton.

Thirty stacks, sixty-four pieces to a stack. One thousand nine hundred and twenty pieces.

After checking the arithmetic, Pangpang looked from the chest to the spot where they had been flattened. This welcome package came in two stages.

They took the mutton to a village. A farmer gave them a house with a large cellar and several rows of empty chests. They stored all thirty stacks downstairs, out of sight of other players.

They had food. Equipment was another matter.

## First Piece: Check the Durability

The next day, fighting broke out by the village entrance.

The three slipped outside and watched from cover. A player called Ali, wearing full netherite armor, was being pursued and had almost no health left.

A golden apple lay on the ground.

Ali threw his spare leggings into the grass, picked up the apple, and threw an Ender pearl. By the time his pursuers arrived, he was gone.

Only after their footsteps faded did Alex retrieve the leggings.

Netherite. Protection IV, Unbreaking III, Mending I, and diamond trim.

Pangpang grew more delighted with every word, until she read the durability too.

“Five points left.”

Steve paused with his hand halfway out.

The equipment had a very long name. Its remaining useful life looked very short.

_A long list of enchantments. Five durability points._

## Second Piece: This One Is Full

On the third day, the village entrance grew noisy again.

Ali again. Pursuers again. The three returned to their hiding place with practiced ease.

A golden apple rolled down the hill. Ali saw it, tossed a chestplate into the grass, grabbed the apple, and vanished with another Ender pearl.

This chestplate had full durability.

Protection II. Thorns III.

Pangpang asked Alex to check again.

“Not Protection IV?”

“Two.”

“Mending?”

“Thorns three.”

He nodded. Yesterday the problem had been durability. Today they needed to listen to the enchantments properly.

Steve laid the pieces together. Diamond trim on the leggings, an intact chestplate beside them. Neither had required the three to draw a sword.

![The three compare an intact netherite chestplate with worn leggings on a workbench while Alex checks the enchantments](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E4%B8%A4%E4%BB%B6%E5%90%88%E9%87%91%E7%94%B2%EF%BC%8C%E7%AC%AC%E4%B8%89%E8%B6%9F%E7%A9%BA%E6%89%8B/scene-2.webp)

_Full durability this time. Listen to the enchantments again._

## Third Trip: Nothing Left Behind

When fighting sounded by the entrance again, they left the house faster than on either previous occasion.

This time Ali wore elytra. A firework carried him upward, and a single attack defeated both pursuers.

Steve and his friends waited in cover.

Ali landed and collected the dropped supplies, piece by piece.

The three kept waiting.

He picked up the last item too.

His elytra opened. A firework sounded. He flew away.

![Ali flies away on elytra while the three emerge from behind a tree to find completely empty grass](/uploads/2026/11/15/%E4%B8%A4%E4%BB%B6%E5%90%88%E9%87%91%E7%94%B2%EF%BC%8C%E7%AC%AC%E4%B8%89%E8%B6%9F%E7%A9%BA%E6%89%8B/scene-3.webp)

_Two trips for armor. A third to watch him collect everything._

Pangpang emerged and inspected the grass.

No leggings.

No chestplate.

Not even a bread crumb.

Alex showed him her empty hands. Time to go home.

Steve glanced back at the spotless village entrance.

The first two times, they had caught Ali escaping. This time, they had caught him tidying up.

The two pieces of netherite armor were still at home.

The third piece had not arrived today.
