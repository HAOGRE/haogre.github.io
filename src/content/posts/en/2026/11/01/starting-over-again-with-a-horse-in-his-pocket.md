_At least this time, the mount could fit in his pocket._

Steve and Alex had settled in the village. For a while, their days had been almost entirely farming.

Finish this row, then the next. Continue tomorrow.

Then TNT fell from the sky.

Neither finished the day's work. Both returned to spawn.

Steve stood there for a moment.

Time to start from zero again.

Alex looked around, as though searching for something that might make the statement less accurate.

Steve began checking his belongings too. Halfway through, he stopped.

There was a spawn egg.

It belonged to the black horse they had found earlier.

This custom world had not left the mount in its usual form. It had turned the horse into something he could store and release.

Steve tried using the egg.

The black horse appeared in front of them.

Alex stepped closer, looking from the horse to Steve.

He tried storing it again. The horse that had just occupied the space became a spawn egg he could hold in his hand.

Release it.

Store it.

He had started by checking what he had left. Now he was repeating the test to make sure.

He had not misread it.

It really was the horse, and it really could be put away.

Steve stored the egg and looked up at Alex.

“At least this can come with us.”

There was still wood to punch and equipment to make. None of the work of starting again had completed itself.

But this time, he would not have to lead the horse along.

He could carry it in his pocket.
