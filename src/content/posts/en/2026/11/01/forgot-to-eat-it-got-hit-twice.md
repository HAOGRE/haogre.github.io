_“I just forgot to eat it.”_

Steve kept feeling that he had forgotten something.

He stood in front of a chest for a while, opened it, and began searching.

Then he found the answer.

Bread.

A great deal of bread.

Food had a shelf life in this custom save. A chest could keep bread stored safely. It could not keep it freshly baked forever.

Steve examined one loaf, then another.

All expired.

He carried the bread to a farmer for confirmation.

The farmer took one look and lost interest in the appraisal.

“You wasted food!”

Before Steve had worked out an answer, the farmer punched him.

Half his health was gone.

Steve looked down at himself, then at the bread. A moment ago, the problem had only been food left too long. Now his health needed attention as well.

He shrugged.

“I just forgot to eat it.”

It was a truthful explanation.

The farmer heard it and hit him again.

What Steve had forgotten originally was the bread.

He had no difficulty remembering those two hits.
