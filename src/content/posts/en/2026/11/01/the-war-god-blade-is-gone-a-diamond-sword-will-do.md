_No divine power. This sword will do for now._

When Steve arrived in this world, he tried using his old powers first.

Nothing happened.

It was an ordinary survival save. Trees were still trees, and grass was still grass. His divine power had not come with him.

Steve stood in front of a tree and raised his fist.

When he had the War God Blade, he had rarely thought seriously about where the first block of wood would come from. Now the question was very practical.

One punch.

Another.

The tree did not hand over its wood simply because he used to be powerful.

Steve kept punching.

The next day, he searched his belongings again. This time, he confirmed that the War God Blade was gone too.

It was not merely a matter of being unable to use its power. The blade itself was missing.

He searched his pack, then searched it again. Finally, he lowered his hands.

“Good. At least that's clear now.”

Later, he found a village and approached the smith. The smith looked at the unarmed traveler and gave him a brand-new diamond sword.

Steve accepted it and studied it for a while.

The sword was new, and its edge was sound. It carried neither his former divine power nor the name he knew so well.

He had meant to grumble about the wretched War God Blade. What came out instead was a rueful laugh.

Even if he still had it, the old blade had little power left now.

This one, at least, was actually in his hand.

Steve thanked the smith and put the diamond sword away. Outside the village, the trees looked just as they had when he arrived.

He would have to punch his own wood and make his tools again.

Someone had helped him with the weapon part first.
