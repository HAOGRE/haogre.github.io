_They had just called it even. The sword now belonged to someone else._

This was another new save.

As soon as Steve arrived, he found a nameplate called “Bad Luck” attached to him. He dug sand. Nothing dropped. He tried again. Still nothing.

The name was accurate.

Later, the plate disappeared.

Steve tried digging another sand block. This time, it dropped. He picked it up and gave a rueful laugh.

“That wretched thing is finally gone.”

Now he could begin properly. Chop a tree, make tools, gradually collect—

Before he had chopped the tree, Alex fell from the sky.

Her landing spot was Steve.

He returned to the nearby respawn point.

Steve ran back and punched Alex. They began a “battle.” The quotation marks were appropriate: Steve had a wooden sword, and the scene was less grand than the word suggested.

A villager passed by and casually gave Steve a diamond sword.

Things changed immediately.

Steve defeated Alex. She respawned and ran back. He looked at her and raised a hand.

“You got me once. I got you once. We're even.”

They had just settled the account when a player appeared from somewhere, snatched the diamond sword, and ran.

Steve paused, then chased alongside Alex.

The stranger was holding the sword that had just been theirs.

One hit each.

Both returned to spawn again.

Once Steve was standing, he looked down to check.

The bad-luck nameplate was definitely gone.

So was the diamond sword.
