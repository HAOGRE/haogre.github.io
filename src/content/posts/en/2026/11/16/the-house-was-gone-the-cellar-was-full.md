Steve heard fighting outside and opened the door only a crack.

More than thirty paying players were demolishing houses in the village. On this Minecraft server, they used their superior gear to search for supplies other people had gathered.

Steve closed the door.

“Downstairs.”

Alex uncovered the cellar entrance. Pangpang went down last. The three squeezed into their equipment store and listened to the noise drawing closer overhead.

![The house is demolished above while the three hide in the cellar below](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E6%88%BF%E5%AD%90%E6%8B%86%E5%85%89%E4%BA%86%EF%BC%8C%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4%E8%A3%85%E6%BB%A1%E4%BA%86/scene-1.webp)

_The house is demolished above while the three hide in the cellar below._

First the walls. Then the roof. Dust sifted through a gap. Pangpang pinched his nose and managed not to sneeze.

The intruders searched the place without finding a single person.

When their footsteps faded, Steve looked out. The house was gone. The cellar remained.

Pangpang stood among the broken bricks and looked back at the entrance.

“They demolished our upstairs.”

“Good thing they didn't move downstairs,” Alex said.

## The Return Route

Later, the three prepared game traps in the block world for the raiders' return.

The cellar was no longer their only answer.

The intruders triggered the mechanisms, and their formation broke apart. Flashes of white followed one another as players returned to spawn, leaving equipment behind. None of the group of more than thirty left with their belongings.

![After the game traps, only equipment drops remain for the three to collect](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E6%88%BF%E5%AD%90%E6%8B%86%E5%85%89%E4%BA%86%EF%BC%8C%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4%E8%A3%85%E6%BB%A1%E4%BA%86/scene-2.webp)

_After the game traps, only equipment drops remain for the three to collect._

Pangpang stood on the safe side, looking over the drops. Before he could stuff them into his pack, Steve pushed a chest toward him.

“Bring everything back.”

They carried the equipment down trip after trip. The house above had been demolished; the room below kept getting more crowded.

## The Part That Took Time

Winning was not the end of the job.

Chestplates with chestplates. Leggings with leggings. Weapons apart from tools. Usable gear within reach; spares farther inside. Alex assembled matching pieces while Pangpang hung them up. Each time he finished one, another appeared behind him.

“More?”

“More.”

![The three sort the crowded cellar's loot and finally clear a walking aisle](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E6%88%BF%E5%AD%90%E6%8B%86%E5%85%89%E4%BA%86%EF%BC%8C%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4%E8%A3%85%E6%BB%A1%E4%BA%86/scene-3.webp)

_The three sort the crowded cellar's loot and finally clear a walking aisle._

Eventually, they left a clear walking space between the racks.

Pangpang sat by the ladder and stared upward.

“They came to demolish our home. Why are we the ones doing all this work?”

Steve put away the last item and shut the chest.

Broken bricks above. Full racks below.

At least tomorrow they could take what they needed without searching through a heap.
