Three days later, a new realm opened: Desert Love.

Creepers in wave one. Creepers in wave two. Creepers again in wave three. More creepers in wave four.

Listening to the noise, Pangpang found less and less love in the name.

Skeletons came later. Wave nine brought an Ender Dragon.

After the fight, stage ten finally presented thirteen treasure chests. Everyone gathered to open them. They contained dragon eggs.

Fake ones.

![The three inspect and replace fake dragon eggs from the desert dungeon chests](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%8F%AB%E4%BC%91%E9%97%B2%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E9%83%BD%E5%85%88%E8%AE%A9%E5%88%AB%E4%BA%BA%E8%AF%95/scene-1.webp)

_The three inspect and replace fake dragon eggs from the desert dungeon chests._

Steve picked one up, inspected it, and put it back. Alex did the same with another.

Pangpang stood nearby without hurrying to open another chest.

“At least these don't explode. Still can't claim a real one.”

## The Casual One

Later, Ali announced another Easter-egg dungeon. A casual dungeon, he said.

Steve, Alex, and Pangpang stayed outside this time and let another party inspect it.

The entering team quickly discovered an Ender Dragon in wave one.

Another Ender Dragon in wave two.

Withers in waves three, four, and five. A Warden waiting in wave six.

![The test party faces a Warden while the three main heroes remain outside](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%8F%AB%E4%BC%91%E9%97%B2%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E9%83%BD%E5%85%88%E8%AE%A9%E5%88%AB%E4%BA%BA%E8%AF%95/scene-2.webp)

_The test party faces a Warden while the three main heroes remain outside._

The party looked at the lineup until someone finally asked the obvious question.

“You call this casual?”

Outside, the three exchanged glances. Pangpang moved half a step back and did not immediately volunteer to try it himself.

## Thirty Newcomers

When Ali made another prank dungeon, Steve sent thirty newcomers inside.

The opening was different this time.

Three zombies in wave one. Four in wave two. Eight in wave three. The early rounds seemed ordinary; the later ones brought the pranks, with mechanisms dropping repeatedly from above.

At stage ten, they found a treasure chest. Inside were nothing but bald caps.

![Newcomers emerge in bald caps as Pangpang checks his own remaining hair](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%8F%AB%E4%BC%91%E9%97%B2%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E9%83%BD%E5%85%88%E8%AE%A9%E5%88%AB%E4%BA%BA%E8%AF%95/scene-3.webp)

_Newcomers emerge in bald caps as Pangpang checks his own remaining hair._

A moment earlier, all thirty had their own hairstyles. Now they could adopt the same gleaming look.

Pangpang watched from outside and touched the top of his own head.

Steve counted people. Alex examined the caps.

“What was the reward this time?” Pangpang asked.

“A hairstyle.”
