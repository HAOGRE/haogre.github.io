Three new characters had barely found their feet in a Minecraft save when a huge crate fell from the sky.

Steve looked up. Alex looked up. Before Pangpang could ask what was inside, all three were back at spawn.

When they returned, the crate was still there.

“Open it first,” Pangpang said. “It's already landed on us once.”

The lid rose. He immediately changed his mind.

“Close it first.”

Everything inside had spoiled. Steve covered his nose, Alex moved upwind, and Pangpang pressed the lid down. They stood around the closed crate in silence.

## A Different Use

This custom save had a special fertilizer rule: this batch of spoiled supplies could be burned down and converted into fertilizer.

Eating it was out of the question. They processed it according to the game's rules and used the resulting fertilizer on nearby soil.

When they returned later, the crops were doing remarkably well.

Steve crouched and pulled up a carrot. Pangpang leaned closer, then glanced back toward the crate.

“Can we eat it this time?”

Alex took the carrot. “This time it's a crop.”

![The three harvest carrots and wheat from the flourishing custom-fertilized field](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E7%AE%B1%E5%AD%90%E4%B8%8D%E8%83%BD%E5%90%83%EF%BC%8C%E5%BA%84%E7%A8%BC%E8%83%BD%E6%8D%A2%E6%88%BF/scene-2.webp)

_The three harvest carrots and wheat from the flourishing custom-fertilized field._

There happened to be a village nearby. They gathered carrots and wheat and carried them over to ask the farmer what they could trade for.

After several trips, they had traded for a house.

Pangpang stood at the entrance, looking at the door, the roof, and his somewhat lighter backpack.

“We have a house now.”

“Bought with crops,” Steve said.

“I know.” Pangpang paused. “I'd rather not trace the transaction any farther back.”

## Traces on the Ground

Before leaving, they returned to the fertilized plot. In this save, they burned over the old field to remove its traces, hoping other players would not discover the place.

Alex checked that nothing had been left behind. Steve counted what remained in their packs.

Three stacks of carrots. Two stacks of wheat.

![The three bring their remaining crops to their traded village house, with the dark old field far away](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E7%AE%B1%E5%AD%90%E4%B8%8D%E8%83%BD%E5%90%83%EF%BC%8C%E5%BA%84%E7%A8%BC%E8%83%BD%E6%8D%A2%E6%88%BF/scene-3.webp)

_The three bring their remaining crops to their traded village house, with the dark old field far away._

That was what they still carried after harvesting and trading.

Back in the village, they opened the door to their new house. Pangpang put his pack down. At last, he could move supplies without holding his nose.

“We're not bringing the crate in, are we?”

Steve shut the door.

“No.”
