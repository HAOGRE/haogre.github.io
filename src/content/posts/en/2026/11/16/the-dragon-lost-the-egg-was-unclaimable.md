Less than three days later, Ali opened a second new realm: Ender City.

Steve entered with Alex, Pangpang, and their party. They found only one ordinary Ender Dragon.

Pangpang tightened his grip on his gear and waited.

“Anything behind it?”

Nothing.

They had expected a tremendous struggle. The actual fight proved fairly easy. The dragon was defeated, and the party finally stood still.

![The ordinary Ender Dragon fades, leaving one egg for the party](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E9%BE%99%E6%89%93%E8%B5%A2%E4%BA%86%EF%BC%8C%E9%BE%99%E8%9B%8B%E6%B2%A1%E6%B3%95%E9%A2%86/scene-1.webp)

_The ordinary Ender Dragon fades, leaving one egg for the party._

## One Dragon Egg

The dragon left an egg.

Black and purple, resting there like the very thing they were supposed to take home.

Steve stepped forward. Alex followed. Pangpang checked for a second dragon, found none, and turned his attention back to the egg.

“Just this?”

This time, yes.

But in this custom realm, the egg was a fusion of three hundred creepers.

![Creeper patterns emerge on the dark egg before the three can claim it](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E9%BE%99%E6%89%93%E8%B5%A2%E4%BA%86%EF%BC%8C%E9%BE%99%E8%9B%8B%E6%B2%A1%E6%B3%95%E9%A2%86/scene-2.webp)

_Creeper patterns emerge on the dark egg before the three can claim it._

## Nothing Came Home

Boom.

The realm was destroyed.

The party that had just been discussing the drop now stood at spawn. Steve paused and looked at his hands. Alex turned to count the others. Pangpang checked his pack first.

They had gained nothing.

They had also made one extra trip back to spawn.

![The dusty three return to spawn with no reward in their hands or packs](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E9%BE%99%E6%89%93%E8%B5%A2%E4%BA%86%EF%BC%8C%E9%BE%99%E8%9B%8B%E6%B2%A1%E6%B3%95%E9%A2%86/scene-3.webp)

_The dusty three return to spawn with no reward in their hands or packs._

After a long moment, Pangpang spoke.

“We did beat the dragon.”

Steve nodded.

“And the egg?”

Alex looked toward where the entrance had been.

“Unclaimable.”
