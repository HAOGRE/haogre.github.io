A player named Ali came to see Steve, Alex, and Pangpang and led them to a tree.

A secret base lay beneath its roots. His shulker boxes held plenty of valuable supplies, including netherite, though enchanted golden apples were relatively scarce.

“Shelter here for now,” Ali said.

Steve inspected his gear: a netherite chestplate, leggings, and boots, all with redstone trims and Protection IV, Unbreaking III, and Mending I.

But no helmet.

![Helmetless Ali guides the three into the secret base beneath the roots](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%A4%B4%E7%9B%94%E6%B2%A1%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%EF%BC%8C%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%E4%B9%9F%E5%A4%9F%E7%94%A8/scene-1.webp)

_Helmetless Ali guides the three into the secret base beneath the roots._

Later, noise came from outside. Their old home had been blown up. The group they had previously defeated with traps was part of the history behind the current pursuit.

Pangpang glanced at the entrance. Nothing revealed their hiding place beneath the tree.

## That One Is Mine

Ali gave each of the three an ordinary netherite set. It had no enchantments, but it would do.

He put on a plain netherite helmet himself and casually handed Steve a bow.

Fully enchanted.

Steve had barely received it when Ali took it back.

“That one's mine.”

He produced another, ordinary bow and gave it to Alex. She looked down and discovered it was made of gold. On this custom server, ordinary did not necessarily mean wooden.

![Ali takes back his enchanted bow as Alex examines her ordinary gold bow](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%A4%B4%E7%9B%94%E6%B2%A1%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%EF%BC%8C%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%E4%B9%9F%E5%A4%9F%E7%94%A8/scene-2.webp)

_Ali takes back his enchanted bow as Alex examines her ordinary gold bow._

Pangpang looked from her gold bow to Ali's own. He did not immediately ask if there were any more.

Steve found a different question.

## Out of Redstone

“Why doesn't your helmet have a trim?”

Red on the chestplate. Red on the leggings and boots. Only the newly donned helmet was plain.

Ali spread his hands.

“Ran out of redstone.”

![Steve asks about the plain helmet while red-trim-armored Ali shrugs](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%A4%B4%E7%9B%94%E6%B2%A1%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%EF%BC%8C%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%E4%B9%9F%E5%A4%9F%E7%94%A8/scene-3.webp)

_Steve asks about the plain helmet while red-trim-armored Ali shrugs._

A short answer. No hidden effect and no special trick. He simply had no redstone left for the trim.

Steve was still looking when Ali climbed outside.

A trident took down one pursuer. A wind charge and mace took down another. Then he spread his elytra and used a spear to defeat three more.

One, another, then three. Five in all.

The larger force fled.

## Back with the Equipment

Steve, Alex, and Pangpang stared for a moment before hearing movement at the entrance again.

Ali carried the collected gear inside, put it before them, and told everyone to share it.

![Ali brings the pursuers' equipment under the tree for everyone to share](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E5%A4%B4%E7%9B%94%E6%B2%A1%E7%BA%B9%E8%B7%AF%EF%BC%8C%E5%87%BA%E9%97%A8%E4%B9%9F%E5%A4%9F%E7%94%A8/scene-4.webp)

_Ali brings the pursuers' equipment under the tree for everyone to share._

This time, there was no need to guess who had won outside. The drops lay at their feet.

Pangpang crouched to sort them. Alex rested her gold bow against the wall. Steve cleared some space.

Ali's helmet still had no redstone trim.

It had done the job for this trip.
