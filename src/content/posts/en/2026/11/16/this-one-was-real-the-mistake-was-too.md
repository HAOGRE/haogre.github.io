Ali made another dungeon. Steve decided to test it himself.

On entering, he found that the first wave was already the ending.

No long walk and no later doors to wait for. A chest stood directly ahead, containing this custom dungeon's Totems of Certain Death.

An ordinary Totem of Undying would save its holder from one lethal hit. Trigger this modified item, and the holder had to return to spawn instead.

Steve tried it, stood outside, and said nothing for a long time.

## All over the Floor

Later, Ali opened another prank dungeon.

Steve led the party himself this time, bringing thirty newcomers. Totems of Undying covered the place. He quickly tested one.

It was the same modified version that had tricked him before.

He looked at the item, then at the waiting team. Thirty newcomers expected his judgment. Steve was growing reluctant to judge these things by appearance alone.

![Steve tests one totem while his newcomers wait among many identical figures](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E8%BF%99%E5%9B%9E%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E6%8B%BF%E9%94%99%E7%9A%84%E4%B9%9F%E6%98%AF/scene-2.webp)

_Steve tests one totem while his newcomers wait among many identical figures._

## A Genuine One Appeared Too

Eventually, he found a stretch of route that looked normal.

Magma blocks covered the ground. Steve went around them and reached a chest full of Totems of Undying.

This time, he checked carefully. They were real.

After taking a genuine one, he continued. Farther ahead lay the old kind of Totem of Certain Death.

They looked similar, and there were plenty of them. With genuine and modified items both around, Steve confused the two.

![After bypassing magma blocks, Steve has a real totem but reaches for an identical modified one](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E8%BF%99%E5%9B%9E%E6%98%AF%E7%9C%9F%E7%9A%84%EF%BC%8C%E6%8B%BF%E9%94%99%E7%9A%84%E4%B9%9F%E6%98%AF/scene-3.webp)

_After bypassing magma blocks, Steve has a real totem but reaches for an identical modified one._

The item he finally triggered was a Totem of Certain Death.

The same spawn point received him again.

He had found a genuine Totem of Undying. He had also made it around the magma blocks.

Looking at his empty hand, he finally managed one sentence.

“This time I know there were real ones.”

He had simply used the wrong one.
