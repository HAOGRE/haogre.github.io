After Ali left to develop dungeons, Steve reached the Freelancers' Home gate one day and found several hundred people waiting outside.

He stopped and looked back. Alex stopped too. Pangpang peered between them, unable to see where the queue ended.

Steve quickly invited everyone in and set aside a large area where they could develop their own homes.

![Steve guides the waiting players toward a large plot where they can build](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E5%AE%9D%E7%AE%B1%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E5%88%AB%E5%BE%80%E5%89%8D%E5%86%B2/scene-1.webp)

_Steve guides the waiting players toward a large plot where they can build._

Once the land was allocated, people streamed in. The quiet place the three knew filled with moving chests, searches for space, and discussions about building houses.

“When the dungeon is ready, we won't be short of teammates,” Pangpang said.

## Only Three Chests Inside

A few days later, Ali opened a dungeon.

The party entered. There was nothing inside except three treasure chests.

Two people rushed forward. A mechanism beneath them triggered, sending both back to spawn before they could open anything.

Everyone else stopped.

![The party stops before three chests after the mechanism triggers, as Ali apologizes](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E5%AE%9D%E7%AE%B1%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E5%88%AB%E5%BE%80%E5%89%8D%E5%86%B2/scene-2.webp)

_The party stops before three chests after the mechanism triggers, as Ali apologizes._

Ali arrived and apologized awkwardly to Steve and the others. It was an old, broken dungeon; a code mix-up had accidentally put it online.

Pangpang looked from the chests to Ali.

“So this wasn't opening-chests practice?”

Ali could only admit the mistake.

## The First Official Dungeon

Eventually, the first official dungeon arrived: a Nether setting with a five-star difficulty rating.

This time there were enemies, and they were difficult. The party fought through and finally defeated the hunter guarding the place before reaching three treasure chests behind it.

Three again.

Steve paused first. Once everyone had checked they could approach, they went together.

![The team collects ancient debris and netherite ingots from three chests](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E5%AE%9D%E7%AE%B1%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E5%88%AB%E5%BE%80%E5%89%8D%E5%86%B2/scene-3.webp)

_The team collects ancient debris and netherite ingots from three chests._

The chests contained ancient debris and netherite ingots.

Nobody returned to spawn this time. They carried the materials home and divided them equally. Holding his share, Pangpang no longer had to guess whether the chests would let them keep anything.

“Still three chests.”

Alex put her materials away. “This time we brought them home.”
