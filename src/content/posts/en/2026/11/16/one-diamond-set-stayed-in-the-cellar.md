On their second morning in the village house, fighting broke out outside.

By the time Steve opened the door, it had stopped. A player's dropped items lay nearby: a complete diamond armor set, a diamond spear, a diamond shovel, a shield, and some golden apples.

Pangpang had just picked up the chestplate when Alex began checking their surroundings.

“Let's not divide it at the front door.”

They quickly dug a cellar and moved the valuables inside. Once everything was stored, there was still only one diamond set.

![The single diamond set, spear, shovel, and shield are stored while the three remain unarmored](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%80%E5%A5%97%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E7%94%B2%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E7%95%99%E5%9C%A8%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4/scene-1.webp)

_The single diamond set, spear, shovel, and shield are stored while the three remain unarmored._

## Three People, One Set

One helmet. One chestplate. One pair of leggings. One pair of boots. Each of the other items was a single piece too.

Pangpang looked from Steve to Alex.

“All on one person?”

Steve put the chestplate back. “Let's equip all three of us first.”

They made iron boots, an iron sword, an iron pickaxe, and a shield for each person, then prepared a bow and sixty-four arrows each. One diamond set did not become three, and two people would not be left empty-handed just so the third could wear it.

They also traded with the farmer for half a stack of emeralds.

![The three prepare iron boots, swords, pickaxes, shields, bows, and arrows](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%80%E5%A5%97%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E7%94%B2%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E7%95%99%E5%9C%A8%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4/scene-2.webp)

_The three prepare iron boots, swords, pickaxes, shields, bows, and arrows._

The diamond armor remained quietly below. When the three went outside again, they had iron under their feet and usable gear in their hands.

## Where Three Diamonds Went

Later, three diamonds suddenly fell from the sky.

Nobody went back to spawn this time. Pangpang crouched to collect them while Steve counted. Two became a diamond sword for Pangpang to wear at his belt.

They traded the remaining diamond.

At this later count, their cloth pouch held twenty-seven emeralds. The proceeds of this trade brought the total to exactly sixty-four and filled the pouch.

“Store this bag first,” Steve said.

![Pangpang wears his diamond sword as Steve takes the full emerald pouch to storage](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%80%E5%A5%97%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E7%94%B2%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E7%95%99%E5%9C%A8%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4/scene-3.webp)

_Pangpang wears his diamond sword as Steve takes the full emerald pouch to storage._

Pangpang checked his new sword and helped carry the full pouch down. They covered the entrance; the ground looked as it had before.

## A Spare Need Not Be Worn

Another noise came from the village entrance.

A player in a full iron set had decided to punch an iron golem with bare hands. His armor was complete. His attacking hand held no weapon.

The golem struck back once. He returned to spawn.

The three hurried over, collected the dropped iron armor, and carried it to the cellar.

![The spare iron set joins the diamond set while the three keep their ordinary gear](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E4%B8%80%E5%A5%97%E9%92%BB%E7%9F%B3%E7%94%B2%EF%BC%8C%E5%85%88%E7%95%99%E5%9C%A8%E5%9C%B0%E4%B8%8B%E5%AE%A4/scene-4.webp)

_The spare iron set joins the diamond set while the three keep their ordinary gear._

Pangpang set it beside the other supplies. Steve checked the equipment they were already wearing.

“Keep it as a spare.”

Alex nodded and straightened the diamond set.

Some gear was worn; some stayed below. The cellar now held one more iron set. That did not mean the group had acquired a fourth person.
