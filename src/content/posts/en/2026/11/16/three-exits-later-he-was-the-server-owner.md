The next day, Ali brought out four pieces of equipment.

Thor's Hammer, the Godslayer Spear, the Tetanus Blade, and his old trident, now named the Fangtian Halberd.

This server did not use ordinary vanilla values. The spear had fourteen base attack; with its fire effect, the custom total was twenty. The Tetanus Blade was a netherite sword with twenty base damage on its own.

Pangpang counted the four items, then the three people receiving them.

“One extra this time.”

## Staying, but Giving It Away

On the third day, Ali said he was not quitting after all.

Alex assumed his personal enchanted bow would remain with him. Instead, he insisted she take it. Unable to refuse, she accepted.

It even had a name: the Sunset Bow.

In this custom world, it could truly shoot down the sun and change the time. Alex held it without drawing at the sky just to test the name.

Pangpang glanced upward.

“Let's leave dinner time where it is.”

![Alex accepts the Sunset Bow while Pangpang checks the sky](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E9%80%80%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%89%E5%9B%9E%EF%BC%8C%E5%8E%9F%E6%9D%A5%E6%98%AF%E6%9C%8D%E4%B8%BB/scene-2.webp)

_Alex accepts the Sunset Bow while Pangpang checks the sky._

On the fourth day, things changed again.

Ali asked for the equipment back and took a complete amethyst-trimmed netherite set from his shulker box. Every piece had Thorns. He put it on, spread his elytra, and flew toward the opposing paid force.

He fought through roughly two hundred or more enemy players. When he returned, he dropped the collected gear on the ground.

Save. Quit.

![Ali flies back in amethyst-trimmed netherite armor and drops collected gear](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E9%80%80%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%89%E5%9B%9E%EF%BC%8C%E5%8E%9F%E6%9D%A5%E6%98%AF%E6%9C%8D%E4%B8%BB/scene-3.webp)

_Ali flies back in amethyst-trimmed netherite armor and drops collected gear._

Pangpang looked at the gear, then at the place where Ali had vanished.

“Didn't he say yesterday he was staying?”

Steve did not rush to answer. He bent down to put the supplies away first.

## Code Can Go Wrong Too

Unhappy that the paying players had lost, an opposing assistant tried to give them cheats. A coding mistake unbanned them instead. They charged in groups and were defeated in groups.

The three patrolled the border, collected the equipment left behind, and carried it to camp.

After the final trip, Steve caught his breath.

“So assistants can make mistakes too.”

## Who Are You, Exactly?

Ali returned again.

The three reacted first with speechless exasperation. Then they finally asked the relevant question.

“Who are you, exactly?”

Ali spread his hands. “All right. No more pretending. I own this server.”

Pangpang's mouth stayed open. Alex looked at Steve. Steve kept looking at Ali.

Ali switched to creative mode to prove it.

![Ali briefly demonstrates creative flight while the three ask him to stop](/uploads/2026/11/16/%E9%80%80%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%89%E5%9B%9E%EF%BC%8C%E5%8E%9F%E6%9D%A5%E6%98%AF%E6%9C%8D%E4%B8%BB/scene-5.webp)

_Ali briefly demonstrates creative flight while the three ask him to stop._

They believed him now and immediately asked him to stop using creative mode. He listened and switched it off.

Later, he explained his plan: stop intervening in survival affairs and develop different dungeons instead.

The three agreed. Ali logged out once more.

Pangpang stared at the empty space for a while.

“At least this time we know what he's gone to do.”
